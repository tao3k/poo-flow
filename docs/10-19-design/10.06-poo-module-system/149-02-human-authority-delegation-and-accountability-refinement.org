#+title: Human Authority delegation and accountability refinement
#+startup: overview

* Status

Lean-proven required refinement of:

- =149-protocol-person-promotion-rfc.org=
- =149-01-cedar-lean-dual-engine-authorization-subject-refinement.org=

RFC 149 assigns Human Audit legitimacy through typed Authority evidence. A
typed role or accepted flag is not yet authority, delegation, or accountable
approval.

* Discovered logical defects

** Unbound authority receipts are replayable

An accepted approval without an authorization subject can be reused across
different requests, policies, entities, Bundles, or epochs.

** Bundle-scoped delegation is insufficient

The initial Lean refinement attempted to scope an authority grant by Bundle
digest. Lean rejected the intended subject-mismatch theorem because two
different authorization subjects legitimately shared the same Bundle.

Authority grants must bind the complete =AuthorizationSubject= defined by RFC
149-01.

** Typed fields do not prove authority

Matching principal, capability, grant ID, subject, and validity interval still
does not prove the authority that issued or validated the receipt. An
engine/provider-specific =AuthorityReceiptValid= relation remains necessary.

** Populated roles do not prove separation of duties

One principal can populate proposer, approver, and executor fields. Non-empty
roles therefore do not establish enterprise accountability.

* Lean countermodels

The compiled theorems establish:

- =unboundAuthorityReplaysAcrossSubjects=: one accepted authority receipt is
  accepted for two different subjects;
- =approvalAuthorityEvidenceClosed=: a correctly scoped, live grant closes the
  authority evidence;
- =revokedAuthorityRejected=: a grant revoked at the promotion epoch is
  rejected;
- =authorityScopeMismatchRejected=: a grant for another complete subject is
  rejected;
- =matchingGrantFieldsDoNotProveAuthorityReceiptValidity=: matching grant fields
  do not prove authority receipt validity;
- =populatedRolesDoNotImplySeparationOfDuties=: one actor can populate all
  roles;
- =humanPromotionClosureRejectsSelfApproval=: strengthened closure rejects that
  self-approval;
- =humanPromotionClosureProvidesBoundAuthority=: accepted promotion evidence
  supplies subject-bound authority evidence.

  revoked_at_epoch

Required capabilities are typed and distinct:

- =propose=;
- =approve=;
- =execute=; and
- =revoke=.

A broad role name such as =admin= does not replace the capability and subject
scope.

The delegation root must identify the authority chain from which the grant was
derived. The grant evidence must be verified by the owning Human Audit or
organization authority contract.

* Refined Human Audit receipt

#+begin_src text
BoundAuthorityReceipt
  receipt_id
  principal
  capability
  authorization_subject
  grant_id
  accepted
  responsibility_acceptance_digest
  authority_signature_or_witness
#+end_src

The responsibility acceptance digest records that the approving principal
accepts the accountable act represented by the exact subject. Authentication
alone is not responsibility acceptance.

* Revocation semantics

For subject epoch =e=:

- an unrevoked grant remains eligible;
- a grant revoked at epoch =r= is eligible only when =e < r=;
- a missing revocation snapshot or ambiguous epoch comparison fails closed;
- revocation authority must itself carry the =revoke= capability and target the
  same grant/delegation chain.

* Separation of duties

The minimal promotion invariant requires pairwise distinct:

- proposer;
- approver; and
- executor.

If an explicitly reviewed enterprise policy permits a role combination, that
exception must be represented as a separate subject-bound policy/authority
receipt. It must not weaken the default invariant implicitly.

* Required rejection rules

The promotion coordinator must reject:

- authority evidence without an authorization subject;
- a grant scoped only by Bundle when the complete subject differs;
- principal mismatch;
- capability mismatch;
- grant ID mismatch;
- subject mismatch;
- approval outside the grant validity interval;
- a grant revoked at or before the subject epoch;
- a missing delegation root;
- matching grant fields without authority receipt validation;
- missing responsibility acceptance;
- proposer/approver identity equality;
- approver/executor identity equality; and
- proposer/executor identity equality.
