The current closure artifact records:

- =ResolvedSourceIdentity.package_id=;
- =ResolvedSourceIdentity.revision=;
- =ResolvedSourceIdentity.source_tree_digest=;
- =verification.okay=;
- =verification.failed_declarations=;
- AXLE request and executor metadata; and
- an =independent_declaration_bundle_digest= sibling field.

It does not record:

- a resolver receipt binding =package_id + revision= to
  =source_tree_digest=; or
- a verification subject binding the AXLE result to the independent bundle
  digest, canonical source digest, roots, and environment.

JSON co-location is not evidence binding. A sibling field can be replaced
without invalidating an unbound receipt.

* Refined resolution contract

Every resolved source identity must be accompanied by a canonical
=ResolutionReceipt=:

#+begin_src text
ResolutionReceipt
  package_id
  revision
  source_tree_digest
  resolver_id
  resolver_receipt_digest
#+end_src

The receipt must be produced or verified by the authority that resolved the
source:

- Lake for Lean dependency resolution;
- the local workspace resolver for active workspace sources; or
- a future package-provider adapter whose receipt contract is separately
  proven.

Merely repeating the claim fields in application code is not a resolver
receipt.

Field equality is also insufficient. Acceptance must invoke the
resolver-specific =ResolverValid= relation over the canonical receipt bytes,
resolver identity, and receipt digest.

* Refined verification contract

Every proof verification receipt must bind:

#+begin_src text
VerificationSubject
  independent_declaration_bundle_digest
  canonical_source_digest
  root_declarations
  axle_environment_identity

BoundVerificationReceipt
  subject
  okay
  failed_declarations
  verifier_identity
  verifier_artifact_digest
  request_identity
#+end_src

Acceptance must recompute the expected subject from the bundle and compare all
subject fields before considering =okay=.

The verifier identity and artifact digest are evidence about who executed the
check. They do not replace the subject binding.

* Required rejection rules

The bundle/verifier boundary must reject:

- a resolved source without a resolution receipt;
- a receipt whose package, revision, or tree digest differs from the claim;
- an unsupported resolver identity;
- a field-matching receipt that fails resolver authority validation;
- a verification receipt without a subject;
- a bundle digest mismatch;
- a canonical source digest mismatch;
- a root declaration mismatch;
- an AXLE environment identity mismatch;
- a verifier artifact identity mismatch when the verifier contract requires a
  pinned artifact; and
- any non-empty failed declaration set even when =okay=true=.
