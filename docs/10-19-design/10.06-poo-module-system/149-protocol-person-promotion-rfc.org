* Decision

A protocol person is an identity-bearing Organization subject that may propose,
accept, reject, execute, and revoke typed commitments only through explicit
protocol transitions.  It is not an anthropomorphic claim and it is not a model
output with direct mutation authority.

The constitutive boundary is:

#+begin_src text
No organizational effect without
  canonical intent
  + current authorization
  + current proof
  + current applicability
  + durable materialization evidence.
#+end_src

=poo-flow= is the semantic compiler and promotion transaction coordinator.
Cedar and Lean are the dual decision engines:

- Cedar answers whether the current principal may request the role transition;
- Lean verifies whether the declared transition satisfies the current proof
  obligations;
- Marlin materializes a transition only after the two decisions join under the
  same Bundle identity and epoch;
- Human Audit supplies required organizational legitimacy for constitutional
  changes through typed Authority evidence, not an ad hoc prompt.

* Research Source And Refinement

[[https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/][Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement]] describes the harness itself as an
optimization target and identifies evaluator quality, memory lifecycle, reward
hacking, long-term success, permission control, and human oversight as open
boundaries.

The generic harness recurrence can be written as:

#+begin_src typst
$
"Inner:" quad c_s^* & = arg max_(c_s) J_"train"(c_s; s) \
"Outer:" quad s^* & = arg max_(s in cal(S)) J_"val"(c_s^*) \
hat(u)(I) & = 1 / abs(cal(D)) sum_((u,x) in cal(D)) u(I(u,x;M)) \
I_t & = I_(t-1)(hat(u), I_(t-1); M)
$
#+end_src

These are the blog's two-level context/harness optimization and recursive
improver shapes.  They explain how to search for a better context function,
skill, or improver.  They do not define when an optimizer-produced artifact may
gain organizational authority.  In an open world, the acceptance boundary must
also bind the evaluator, authorization policy, proof specification, and
environment to a current Organization epoch.

This RFC refines the recurrence to a role-promotion transaction over one
Canonical Organization Bundle.

* Owner Boundary

| Owner | Normative responsibility |
|-
| Organization Bundle | Canonical identities, roles, epochs, lineage, context, protocol state, and evidence roots. |
| Scheme/POO control plane | POO-native authoring, functional composition, validation, =PromotionIntent= construction, obligation selection, and decision join. |
| Cedar | Authorization decision over current principal, action, resource, context, policy, entities, and Authority epoch. |
| Lean | Verification of declared promotion, epoch, amendment, temporal-role, and materialization invariants. |
| Human Audit / quorum | Legitimacy for constitutional changes according to the old/current Authority epoch. |
| Marlin/runtime kernel | Atomic claim, lease, materialization, injection, receipt persistence, rollback, and indeterminate-outcome containment. |

POO/C3 composition determines inherited definition precedence.  It does not
grant authority and it does not prove promotion safety.

* Canonical Promotion Model

A =PromotionIntent= binds:

- promotion identity and candidate content digest;
- source role, target role, and requested scope;
- expected Bundle, Authority, proof, and evaluator epochs;
- Cedar policy/entity/decision identities;
- Lean theorem-set and proof-specification identities;
- evidence roots and provenance;
- invalidation and revocation triggers;
- materialization idempotency key;
- injection target and projection identity;
- rollback target and postconditions.

The first supported role transition is:

#+begin_src text
CandidateSkill
  -> Proposed
  -> Authorized
  -> Verified
  -> Reserved
  -> Materialized
  -> Active
  -> Revoked / History
#+end_src

Rejected, denied, refuted, stale, superseded, and revoked candidates remain
historical evidence.  They do not remain in the active harness projection.

* Mathematical Contract

The equations below are normative mathematical presentations.  The stable IDs
bind them to Lean definitions and theorems in the next section.

#+name: protocol-person-promotion-math
#+begin_src typst
$
// PP-M01: epoch agreement
"EpochMatch"(i, w) & equiv
  i."bundle" = w."bundle"
  and i."authority" = w."authority"
  and i."proof" = w."proof"
  and i."evaluator" = w."evaluator" \

// PP-M02: dual-engine admission
"Approved"(i, w, d) & equiv
  "CedarPermit"(d)
  and "LeanVerified"(d)
  and "EvaluatorApplicable"(d)
  and "EvidenceComplete"(d)
  and "EpochMatch"(i, w) \

// PP-M03: active means approved and durably projected
"Active"(i, w, d) & equiv
  "Approved"(i, w, d)
  and "MaterializedOnce"(d)
  and "InjectionReceipted"(d) \

// PP-M04: a new constitution cannot approve itself
"Amend"(B_e, B_(e+1)) & equiv
  "CedarPermit"_e(B_e, B_(e+1))
  and "LeanVerified"_e(B_e, B_(e+1))
  and "RequiredQuorum"_e
  and "TransitionReceipt"(B_e, B_(e+1)) \

// PP-M05: exactly-once materialization by promotion identity
"Materialize"(p, bot) & = ("Applied", p) \
"Materialize"(p, p) & = ("AlreadyApplied", p) \
p != q => "Materialize"(p, q) & = ("Conflict", q) \

// PP-M06: history is monotonic; active applicability is retractable
"ActiveProjection"(x, t) & equiv
  "Admitted"(x)
  and not "Superseded"(x, t)
  and not "Revoked"(x, t)
  and "Applicable"(x, t) \
"ActiveProjection"(x, t) & => "HistoricallyAdmitted"(x)
$
#+end_src

The refined harness recurrence is therefore:

#+begin_src typst
$
// PP-M07: optimizer output changes the active harness only after promotion
h_(t+1) = cases(
  "Project"(B_(e+1), t+1) & "if Active"(i, w_t, d_t),
  h_t & "otherwise"
)
$
#+end_src

Failure to promote does not delete the candidate.  It retains the candidate and
its counter-evidence in history while leaving the current active projection
unchanged.

* Cedar Promotion Projection

The Cedar request must be derived from the same =PromotionIntent= that selects the
Lean obligations:

#+begin_src text
principal = current Organization subject
action    = promote(source-role, target-role)
resource  = candidate identity + requested scope
context   = Bundle/Authority/proof/evaluator epochs + evidence roots
#+end_src

A Cedar =Permit= without a verified Lean obligation set is not sufficient.  A
verified Lean proof without Cedar =Permit= is not sufficient.  Identity or epoch
divergence between the two projections rejects the join.

* Constitutional Amendment Rule

Changes to any of the following are constitutional:

- Cedar authorization policy or entity interpretation;
- Lean specification, trusted theorem set, or proof-obligation selection;
- evaluator identity, objective, or promotion threshold;
- Authority graph, quorum, emergency principal, or promotion rule.

The new epoch cannot authorize or prove its own installation.  The old/current
epoch evaluates the cross-epoch transition, including required human/quorum
evidence.  Marlin may materialize the new epoch only after the transition receipt
binds both old and new identities.

* Materialization And Injection Contract

Marlin must expose deterministic terminal states:

| State | Meaning |
|-
| =Applied= | The promotion identity was atomically materialized for the first time. |
| =AlreadyApplied= | The same promotion identity was previously materialized; return the same canonical receipt. |
| =Conflict= | Another promotion identity owns the target materialization slot. |
| =Indeterminate= | An external effect may have occurred but observation or durable root commit is incomplete; do not retry automatically. |

An active transition requires both a materialization receipt and an injection
receipt.  The injection receipt binds the active projection digest, ordered source
identities, target actor/model/session, Bundle epoch, and exactly-once injection
identity.

* Human Audit Boundary

Human Audit is triggered by semantic privilege, not by a fixed percentage of
agent turns.

| Change | Required path |
|-
| Operational action inside a current capability lease | Existing Cedar/Lean/runtime gate; no mandatory per-turn human review. |
| Reversible Skill or Workflow promotion | Cedar + Lean; human/quorum only when selected by current risk policy. |
| Evaluator, Authority, Cedar policy, Lean specification, or promotion-rule change | Old/current epoch plus mandatory Human Audit or configured quorum. |
| Emergency revocation | Current constitution's emergency principal, followed by durable audit and reconciliation. |

* Acceptance Criteria

This RFC's first slice is accepted only when:

- Org lint passes for this file and the linked research report;
- =lake build= compiles =ProtocolPersonPromotion.lean= through the root proof
  package;
- the positive dual-engine construction theorem compiles;
- Cedar deny, unverified proof, stale epoch, missing injection, revocation,
  supersession, conflict, and new-epoch self-approval negative theorems compile;
- the report links to this normative RFC;
- no Scheme, Cedar, Marlin, C ABI, or generated-manifest implementation is claimed
  complete by this proof-only slice.

* Non-Goals

- proving that a model is conscious or legally a human person;
- proving scientific truth from authorization and type-level facts;
- treating a Markdown, JSON, Org, or vector-store write as promotion;
- allowing C3 precedence to grant authority;
- allowing a new evaluator, Cedar policy, or Lean specification to self-approve;
- freezing Bundle v1 or the public ABI in this slice.
