* Ownership and open bridge

The finite contract uses caller-owned symbol IDs and exact nonnegative
positions in a declared clock. A complete local answer does not authenticate
that clock, cut, source coverage or a claimed domain causal mechanism. A
hypothetical fork requires separately named source/cut inputs and retains its
hypothetical scope. Existing POO causal-event, causal-cut, revision and evidence
contracts continue to own source authority and domain-neutral assessment.

No automatic mapping from a POO causal-cut object's wire identity into this
finite lens is implemented. That mapping needs an explicit source-owner
clock/cut/admission contract with MRR. Interval uncertainty, generic temporal
fragment composition, Provider-update failure handling, broader scale and
release/deployment authority remain separate gates. The existing approved
four-call repair study stays bound to its earlier ASCENT =cd6f60f= and POO
=0b7a6c52=; this dependency update neither changes its frozen preview nor
spends any model-call budget. No model calls were made for this slice.
