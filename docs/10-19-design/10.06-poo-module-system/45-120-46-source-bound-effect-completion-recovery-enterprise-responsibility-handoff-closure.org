* Decision

RFC 45-120-46 defines one canonical v1 adjacent-responsibility relation over
two active recovery-trace positions.

Let =A_i= and =A_(i+1)= be the responsibility assignments selected by the
exact commitments at adjacent positions.

#+begin_src typst
$ C(A_i, A_(i + 1), tau_i) :=
  S_"stable"(A_i, A_(i + 1))
  or H_"handoff"(A_i, A_(i + 1), tau_i) $
#+end_src

The two branches are exclusive:

- stable continuity requires the same accountable principal, subject, and
  responsibility scope;
- handoff continuity requires different accountable principals and a transfer
  admitted by the existing =Enterprise.ResponsibilityTransfer= owner.

The stable branch does not require a transfer. The handoff branch cannot be
constructed for an unchanged principal.

* Reused owners

This RFC introduces no second transfer model.

| Existing owner | Reused fact |
|-
| RFC 45-120-45 | each active position selects a valid responsibility assignment |
| =Enterprise.ResponsibilityTransfer.Transfer= | principal transition, transfer contract, authority evidence, acceptance evidence, effective observation, gap state, and exclusive-authority state |
| =Enterprise.ResponsibilityTransfer.Admitted= | exact contract, transfer authority, successor acceptance, current observation, no gap, and no overlapping exclusive authority |

The new owner is only the adjacency bridge:
=SourceBoundEffectCompletionRecoveryEnterpriseResponsibilityAdjacentContinuity=.

* Lean-found architecture gaps

** Two valid assignments do not imply a handoff

The independent model constructs two valid assignments with the same subject,
scope, and epoch, but with different accountable principals and no transfer.

#+begin_src typst
$ V(A_i)
  and V(A_(i + 1))
  and A_i.s = A_(i + 1).s
  and A_i.rho = A_(i + 1).rho
  and A_i.a != A_(i + 1).a
  and not C(A_i, A_(i + 1), emptyset) $
#+end_src

The theorem
=validAdjacentAssignmentsDoNotImplyResponsibilityHandoff= proves this
counterexample. Per-position validity cannot establish continuity between
positions.

** Generic transfer admission does not identify the adjacent commitments

The existing generic transfer has =exactTransferContract : Prop= and a
=transferContractIdentity=. Its type does not name the two commitments selected
by this recovery trace.

A bare proof of =exactTransferContract= therefore cannot establish that the
contract governs =A_i= and =A_(i+1)=.

#+begin_src typst
$ B_"contract"(
    tau_i.k,
    A_i.c,
    A_(i + 1).c,
  ) $
#+end_src

The actual wrapper consumes the owner-supplied relation
=transferContractBinds=. The bridge never infers this relation from a nonempty
contract identity.

** A current observation proposition does not identify its epoch

The generic transfer also has =effectiveObservationCurrent : Prop= and an
effective-observation identity. Without an explicit relation, the proposition
does not prove which recovery epoch was observed.

#+begin_src typst
$ B_"observation"(tau_i.o, A_(i + 1).epsilon) $
#+end_src

The actual wrapper consumes =effectiveObservationBinds= and binds the
observation identity to the successor assignment's effective epoch.

** Authority and acceptance evidence must resolve to the successor assignment

The transfer's authority evidence and acceptance evidence cannot float
independently of the successor assignment.

#+begin_src typst
$ tau_i.h = A_(i + 1).r
  and tau_i.xi = A_(i + 1).xi $
#+end_src

Here =A.r= is the bound authority-receipt identity and =A.xi= is the accepted
assignment evidence identity.

** Subject and scope changes are not responsibility handoff

A responsibility handoff changes the accountable principal. It does not change
the authorization subject or responsibility scope.

#+begin_src typst
$ C(A_i, A_(i + 1), tau_i)
  => A_i.s = A_(i + 1).s
  and A_i.rho = A_(i + 1).rho $
#+end_src

A subject or scope change is a new assignment-domain transition and requires a
separate proof owner. It must not be hidden inside responsibility handoff.

* Canonical stable branch

#+begin_src typst
$ S_"stable"(A_i, A_(i + 1)) :=
  A_i.a = A_(i + 1).a
  and A_i.s = A_(i + 1).s
  and A_i.rho = A_(i + 1).rho $
#+end_src

The transfer registry may contain an inert value at a stable position, but that
value contributes no semantic evidence. Only the stable equalities discharge
the branch.

* Canonical handoff branch

For transfer =tau_i=:

#+begin_src typst
$ H_"handoff"(A_i, A_(i + 1), tau_i) :=
  A_i.a != A_(i + 1).a
  and D_"admitted"(tau_i)
  and tau_i.p = A_i.a
  and tau_i.q = A_(i + 1).a
  and tau_i.s = A_i.s = A_(i + 1).s
  and tau_i.rho = A_i.rho = A_(i + 1).rho
  and B_"contract"(tau_i.k, A_i.c, A_(i + 1).c)
  and tau_i.h = A_(i + 1).r
  and tau_i.xi = A_(i + 1).xi
  and B_"observation"(tau_i.o, A_(i + 1).epsilon) $
#+end_src

=Admitted(tau_i)= remains the existing generic predicate:

#+begin_src typst
$ D_"admitted"(tau_i) :=
  tau_i.e_"contract"
  and tau_i.e_"authority"
  and tau_i.e_"accepted"
  and tau_i.e_"current"
  and not tau_i.g_"gap"
  and not tau_i.g_"overlap" $
#+end_src

The wrapper adds domain binding; it does not redefine generic admission.

* Recovery-trace closure

For adjacent positions that are both non-committed:

#+begin_src typst
$ forall i,
  T(i) != q_"committed"
  and T(i + 1) != q_"committed"
  => C(
    Q(E(i).c),
    Q(E(i + 1).c),
    tau(i),
  ) $
#+end_src

The RFC 45-120-45 evidence remains embedded, so both assignments are already
bound to their exact audit commitments, Cedar evidence, authority receipts,
subjects, scopes, and recovery epochs.

* Next proof obligation

The current closure indexes transfers by adjacent trace position. It does not
yet prove global nonreuse of:

- transfer contract identities;
- acceptance evidence identities;
- effective observation identities.

A replayed identity could satisfy two owner relations unless the owner relations
themselves enforce global uniqueness. The next model must decide which identities
may be intentionally shared and which must be injective over distinct handoffs.
No global nonreuse claim is made by this RFC.
