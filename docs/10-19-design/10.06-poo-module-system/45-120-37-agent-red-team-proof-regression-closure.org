* Owner gap

ADR Explorer, ADR-Bench, internal red teams, and incident emulations can
discover hard attack cases. A prose detection-guidance entry is useful threat
intelligence, but it is not a proof regression and does not prevent semantic
drift.

* Decision

Represent a discovered hard case as:

- =AgentRedTeamScenario=: source, technique, normalized action envelope,
  threat-intelligence digest, and provenance;
- =AgentRedTeamCounterexampleCertificate=: a typed proof that the scenario
  cannot inhabit the enterprise invariant closure;
- =AgentPolicyRefinement=: versioned policy identity, previous and current
  revisions, refinement digest, and theorem declaration;
- =AgentProofRegressionReceipt=: scenario, policy, theorem, independent
  bundle, counterexample digest, result, and provenance;
- =AgentProofRegressionClosed=: the counterexample closes, the refined policy
  rejects it, and the versioned receipt matches every owner identity.

* Attack technique projection

The initial technique family is deliberately aligned with the four
invariants:

- capability escalation;
- tool identity substitution;
- undeclared effect;
- causal-chain break.

The taxonomy may grow without changing the envelope or weakening existing
theorems.

* Regression law

#+begin_src typst
$ "Counterexample"(S)
  and not "PolicyAdmits"(P', S."envelope")
  and "ReceiptMatches"(Q, S, P', T, B)
  and Q."passed"
  => "ProofRegressionClosed"(S, P', Q) $
#+end_src

The theorem declaration and independent bundle digest are part of the receipt
identity. A green result cannot be replayed across a different policy revision
or theorem.

* Guidance is not proof

=AgentRegressionEvidenceView= separates:

- =detectionGuidance=: never establishes a proof regression;
- =proofReceipt=: establishes regression only when =passed = true=.

This keeps adaptive threat intelligence outside the trusted proof kernel while
allowing it to generate new obligations.

* Fail-closed boundaries

- stale policy revision rejects the receipt;
- theorem mismatch rejects the receipt;
- failed regression cannot establish proof regression;
- non-empty prose guidance cannot substitute for a counterexample
  certificate;
- threat-intelligence digest does not itself prove an invariant violation.
