* Problem

RFC 55 requires every verifier to check context membership against one
authority-owned fork-free publication domain.  Membership establishes that a
context was published.  It does not establish that the context is the current
head.

A valid domain normally retains multiple generations:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    "Member"(D, C_0)
      and
    "Member"(D, C_1)
      and
    C_0 != C_1
  $
]
#+end_src

Both propositions may be true while only =C_1= is current.  Treating
publication membership as currentness permits historical-context substitution
even though the domain is fork-free.

* Authority publication checkpoint

An authority-issued checkpoint selects exactly one member of publication
domain =D= as its current head:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    H : "Checkpoint"(D)
    quad "and" quad
    "Member"(D, "head"(H))
  $
]

#align(center)[
  $
    "CurrentAt"(H, C)
      "iff"
    C = "head"(H)
  $
]
#+end_src

The checkpoint is parameterized by its publication domain.  Evidence from
another domain is not interchangeable with evidence checked against =D=.

* Current-head uniqueness

Two candidates proven current at the same checkpoint are equal:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    "CurrentAt"(H, C_l)
      and
    "CurrentAt"(H, C_r)
      "implies"
    C_l = C_r
  $
]
#+end_src

A historical domain member that is not the checkpoint head is rejected:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    "Member"(D, C_h)
      and
    C_h != "head"(H)
      "implies"
    not "CurrentAt"(H, C_h)
  $
]
#+end_src

This separates two evidence questions:

1. Was this context published by the authority?
2. Is this published context the authority's current head?

Receipt-DAG admission requires both answers.

* Checkpoint advancement

A checkpoint successor is valid only when its head transition satisfies RFC
55:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    "ValidCheckpointAdvance"(H_i, H_j)
      "iff"
    "ValidTransition"("head"(H_i), "head"(H_j))
  $
]
#+end_src

A receipt accepted at =head(H_i)= is rejected at =head(H_j)= after a valid
advance.

* Watermark evidence is bounded

A verifier may retain a trusted per-authority minimum generation =W=.  It can
reject a checkpoint whose head is older than the verifier's observed
high-water mark:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    g("head"(H)) < W(A)
      "implies"
    not "WatermarkAccepted"(W, H)
  $
]
#+end_src

This detects a known rollback.  It does not prove that an accepted checkpoint
is globally latest:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    "WatermarkAccepted"(W, H_h)
      and
    H_h != H_"live"
  $
]
#+end_src

The countermodel uses a verifier whose watermark has not yet observed the live
head generation.  The historical checkpoint clears the local lower bound while
remaining different from the live authority checkpoint.

Watermark evidence is therefore source-scoped bounded evidence, not an
absolute-currentness oracle.

* Declared head matching is not authority issuance

A declared checkpoint attestation is data.  A candidate can structurally match
that declaration and clear a low watermark:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    "MatchesDeclared"(W, H_d, H)
      "iff"
    "WatermarkAccepted"(W, H)
      and
    "head"(H) = "head"(H_d)
  $
]
#+end_src

A historical checkpoint can declare itself as =H_d=.  The equality then holds
by construction while the actual current checkpoint is different.  A
structure named “live attestation” would not close this gap.

* Live authority-issued admission

The live authority owns an issuance predicate and proves that every issued
attestation names its actual current checkpoint:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    "Issued"_A(H_d)
      "implies"
    "head"(H_d) = "head"("CurrentCheckpoint"(A))
  $
]

#align(center)[
  $
    "AdmitCheckpoint"(W, A, H_d)
      "iff"
    "Issued"_A(H_d)
      and
    "WatermarkAccepted"(W, H_d)
  $
]
#+end_src

A valid historical checkpoint remains auditable offline.  Without live-head
evidence, an offline verifier must not upgrade historical validity into a claim
of current admission.

* Ownership boundary

- The authority-owned publication domain proves publication membership.
- The authority-issued checkpoint selects the current domain head.
- The verifier-owned watermark rejects rollback below a previously observed
  authority generation.
- A declared checkpoint attestation has no authority status by construction
  alone.
- The live authority owner supplies an issuance proof that binds the
  attestation to its actual current checkpoint.
- The receipt-DAG verifier requires membership and exact checkpoint-head
  equality.
- Historical members remain auditable but are not current.
- Audit JSON records checkpoint evidence and never selects or advances the
  head.
- Checkpoint advancement inherits the RFC 55 same-authority monotone
  generation rule.
- Separate publication domains and their checkpoints are not unioned.

* State machine

#+begin_src mermaid
stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> PublishedHistorical: context C0 belongs to domain D
  PublishedHistorical --> PublishedCurrent: context C1 belongs to domain D
  PublishedCurrent --> CheckpointIssued: authority selects C1 as head
  CheckpointIssued --> WatermarkChecked: candidate is not below observed generation
  WatermarkChecked --> DeclaredMatched: candidate equals declared head
  DeclaredMatched --> SelfReportRejected: authority issuance is absent
  DeclaredMatched --> AuthorityIssued: live authority issued declaration
  AuthorityIssued --> CurrentAccepted: bounded and authority evidence agree
  WatermarkChecked --> StaleCheckpointRejected: candidate differs from declared head
  CheckpointIssued --> HistoricalRejected: candidate is member but not head
  CurrentAccepted --> CheckpointAdvanced: authority publishes a valid successor
  CheckpointAdvanced --> PriorReceiptRejected: prior head is stale
  StaleCheckpointRejected --> [*]: live-head evidence required
  SelfReportRejected --> [*]: authority issuance required
  HistoricalRejected --> [*]: current-head evidence required
  PriorReceiptRejected --> [*]: revalidation required
#+end_src

* Architectural consequence

Fork-free publication membership is necessary but insufficient for current
receipt admission.  The complete rule is:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    "AdmitCurrent"(r, H)
      "requires"
    "Member"(D, "issued"(r))
      and
    "issued"(r) = "head"(H)
      and
    "CheckpointValid"(H, D)
      and
    "Issued"_A(H)
  $
]
#+end_src

This preserves historical auditability while preventing a previously
published context from being substituted for the authority's current head.
