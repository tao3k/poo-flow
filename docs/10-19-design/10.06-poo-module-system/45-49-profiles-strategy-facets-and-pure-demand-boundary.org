#+title: Profiles Strategy Facets and Pure Demand Boundary
#+subtitle: Construction, demand resolution, evidence projection, and externally supplied live evaluation
#+startup: overview

* RFC status

- Status :: Accepted design
- Kind :: Normative composition-strategy protocol
- Semantic core :: Native =gerbil-poo= objects and POO Flow evaluation
- Related modules :: =45-38-poo-observability-profile-projection-retention-and-redaction.org= and =45-48-compose-function-profiles-strategy-and-lazy-evaluator-boundary.org=

This module divides the responsibilities of the first-class =profiles= POO strategy into three normative facets while preserving one higher-order POO object and one native object-extension path.

* Core decision

#+begin_quote
=profiles= is one higher-order POO strategy object with construction, demand-resolution, and evidence facets. A pure composition value retains only pure strategy projections. A live evaluation context is always supplied externally.
#+end_quote

The facets are semantic projections of one object. They are not three registries, three plugin systems, or three new object DSLs.

Concrete slots and native operators must be selected only after verification against the real =gerbil-poo= interface.

* Construction facet

The construction facet defines:

- strategy semantic identity;
- the empty composition identity element;
- structural admission of operands;
- the distinction among a Profile, an anchored root, and a nested composition;
- same-strategy unanchored normalization;
- explicit admission or conversion across different strategies;
- canonical definition-root projection;
- structural rejection evidence.

It does not:

- demand a business slot;
- validate a live capability token;
- create an evaluation session;
- execute a fixed point;
- read cache, clock, worker, or runtime environment state.

Construction returns a pure composition value and does not perform runtime handoff.

* Demand-resolution facet

The demand-resolution facet defines the pure rules for resolving one semantic slot:

- canonical semantic-slot reference identity;
- contribution enumeration rules;
- identity, contract, and capability-requirement projection;
- inherited and slot-local priority;
- candidate-admission policy;
- equal-priority canonical-combination policy;
- conflict classification;
- dependency-demand expansion rules;
- resolver-policy references.

The facet defines policy rather than live execution:

- The strategy defines capability requirements and admission rules.
- The evaluator verifies an actual token or lease.
- The strategy defines the semantic dependency and fixed-point rules.
- The evaluator executes demands, discovers SCCs, and enforces budgets.
- The strategy classifies conflict.
- The live recovery portal validates recovery authority.

The demand-resolution facet may produce pure demand descriptors or a pure demand plan. It does not return a live session handle.

* Evidence facet

The evidence facet defines pure projection and explanation rules for:

- composition construction;
- normalization;
- demand and candidate reasoning;
- priority winners, shadows, and conflicts;
- identity and provenance links;
- invalidation explanations;
- mandatory causal fields;
- redaction-safe opaque identity projection.

The facet does not retain:

- a mutable event log;
- logger or clock handles;
- continuation or resumption;
- live recovery portal;
- worker or scheduler;
- capability token or lease.

The evaluator materializes receipts from evaluation events according to the evidence projection. The strategy object does not become a mutable observability service.

* Correction to the demand-interface wording

A pure composition value does not contain a callable live evaluator handle.

It may retain:

- strategy semantic projection;
- operand and root identities;
- a pure demand-policy projection;
- a pure evidence-policy projection;
- the canonical definition graph.

It does not retain:

- evaluator instance;
- cache client;
- worker pool;
- logger or clock;
- capability token;
- continuation or recovery portal;
- mutable session state.

The conceptual evaluation boundary is:

#+begin_src text
evaluate(
  PureCompositionValue,
  PureSlotDemand,
  LiveEvaluationContext
)
#+end_src

It is not:

#+begin_src text
composition.evaluate-with-hidden-runtime()
#+end_src

This correction narrows the phrase "demand interface" used in the composition-boundary RFC. The value retains pure demand semantics, not a live service object.

* Pure slot demand

A pure slot demand identifies:

- semantic generation;
- semantic slot reference;
- demand root;
- pure evaluation-policy identity;
- requested evidence projection;
- declared budget-policy identity.

It excludes:

- remaining live budget counters;
- wall-clock deadline;
- worker or scheduler;
- live capability or lease;
- continuation and resumption;
- mutable execution state.

One pure demand may be executed by multiple sessions and still share one semantic result identity.

* Strategy identity separation

A strategy has at least three projections:

#+begin_src text
StrategySemanticIdentity
StrategyEvidenceProjectionIdentity
StrategyImplementationIdentity
#+end_src

** Strategy semantic identity

This identity includes construction and demand-resolution behavior capable of changing:

- canonical composition meaning;
- candidate admission;
- priority result;
- conflict result;
- semantic dependency graph;
- recovery-policy availability.

It participates in semantic generation identity.

** Strategy evidence projection identity

This identity includes:

- evidence schema;
- explanation projection;
- retention and redaction policy;
- optional evidence materialization rules.

It does not alter the business result identity merely because evidence presentation changes.

If an apparent evidence rule changes candidate admission, recovery authority, or business behavior, that rule is semantic or control-plane policy and must move into =StrategySemanticIdentity=.

** Strategy implementation identity

This identity describes operational implementation:

- scheduler;
- optimization;
- instrumentation;
- worker topology;
- cache transport;
- runtime adapter implementation.

It may change execution receipts and performance. It must not change observable semantic results.

#+begin_src mermaid

    C["Construction semantic projection"] -->|"changes composition meaning"| S["StrategySemanticIdentity"]
    D["Demand-resolution policy"] -->|"changes slot result or conflict"| S
    E["Evidence schema, retention, and redaction"] -->|"changes proof materialization only"| P["StrategyEvidenceProjectionIdentity"]
    I["Scheduler, optimization, and instrumentation"] -->|"changes execution only"| X["StrategyImplementationIdentity"]

#+end_src

* Cache boundaries

Semantic result caching uses:

#+begin_src text
SemanticGenerationIdentity
+ PureSlotDemand semantic identity
+ semantic evaluator protocol identity
#+end_src

Evidence caching additionally uses the evidence projection identity.

Operational execution caching, if any, remains separate and cannot prove semantic equivalence.

Changing only:

- redaction;
- retention;
- presentation;
- instrumentation;
- worker topology;

does not unconditionally invalidate a business result. It may require new evidence materialization or a new execution receipt.

If required proof material is unavailable under the new evidence projection, the system may conservatively reevaluate to produce evidence. That operational need does not imply that the semantic result identity changed.

* Facet extension

An advanced strategy extends one POO strategy object through native prototype operations.

Facet extension:

- does not create a public macro;
- does not modify =compose=;
- does not create a facet registry;
- preserves the contracts of the other facets;
- changes strategy semantic identity when semantic behavior changes;
- changes evidence projection identity when only evidence behavior changes;
- changes implementation identity when only operational implementation changes.

The final construction must use verified =gerbil-poo= primitives. The conceptual facets must not become raw records, alists, callback tables, or an independent class hierarchy.

* Facet coherence

The three facets must remain coherent:

- Construction produces values accepted by demand resolution.
- Demand resolution emits events understood by evidence projection.
- Evidence projection cannot reinterpret or alter demand results.
- Implementation cannot change semantic behavior while retaining the same semantic identity.
- A semantic extension declares the evidence fields needed to explain its behavior.
- A new evidence projection cannot grant semantic or recovery authority.

An incoherent strategy fails structural admission before business-slot demand.

* Evidence requirements

Strategy-facet evidence records:

- strategy semantic identity;
- construction-facet projection identity;
- demand-resolution projection identity;
- evidence projection identity;
- implementation identity where operationally relevant;
- facet-extension lineage;
- coherence validation;
- cache namespace selected for semantic result, evidence, and execution;
- proof that no live evaluator handle entered the pure composition value.

* Implementation checklist

- [ ] Define construction, demand-resolution, and evidence as projections of one POO strategy object.
- [ ] Verify that no independent facet registry or object DSL is introduced.
- [ ] Define pure construction and demand-resolution protocol requirements.
- [ ] Define pure evidence projection and explanation requirements.
- [ ] Remove any live evaluator handle from pure composition-value design.
- [ ] Define pure =SlotDemand= identity and projection.
- [ ] Supply live evaluation context only at the explicit evaluator boundary.
- [ ] Separate strategy semantic, evidence, and implementation identities.
- [ ] Key business results independently from evidence presentation and runtime implementation.
- [ ] Revalidate proof availability without changing semantic identity.
- [ ] Validate facet coherence before business-slot demand.
- [ ] Verify that evidence rules cannot grant admission or recovery authority.
- [ ] Verify that implementation changes preserve observable semantic results.
- [ ] Generate facet, coherence, and cache-selection evidence.
- [ ] Validate final facet projection against real =gerbil-poo= primitives.

* Non-goals

- Do not create three strategy registries or plugin APIs.
- Do not place a live evaluator inside a pure composition value.
- Do not let evidence presentation alter business semantic identity.
- Do not let implementation identity alter semantic cache keys.
- Do not retain logger, clock, capability, continuation, or worker handles in pure strategy projection.
- Do not create raw record, alist, callback-table, or class DSLs.
- Do not turn facet extension into public macro extension.
