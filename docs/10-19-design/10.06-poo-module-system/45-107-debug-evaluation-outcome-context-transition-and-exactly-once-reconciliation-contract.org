* Scope

This RFC fixes the POO-native value algebra returned by one admitted debug
evaluation demand.  It defines:

- terminal evaluation outcomes;
- immutable input-to-successor context transitions;
- exactly-once logical reconciliation;
- budget, cleanup, cache, and publication receipts;
- the boundary between a terminal condition and local continuable handling;
- the rule that =Resume= is a new coordinator admission.

This RFC does not define:

- a new exception system;
- a replacement for =gerbil-poo=;
- a second fixed-point implementation;
- a persistence engine;
- a public outcome macro;
- physical exactly-once execution of side effects.

* Central decision

An admitted evaluation is a pure transition at the control-plane boundary:

#+begin_example
EvaluationContext × EvaluationDemand
  -> EvaluationOutcome × SuccessorEvaluationContext
#+end_example

The input context is never mutated.  Every terminal path returns one
=EvaluationOutcome= and one successor context that accounts for all work
performed by that demand.

=EvaluationOutcome= is a native POO object family.  It is not an alist, record
DSL, status string, exception tuple, or keyword-clause language.

* Evaluation identity

Every admitted execution has distinct identities for:

- =DemandIdentity=: the semantic request;
- =DemandExecutionIdentity=: one admitted attempt;
- =InputContextIdentity=: the immutable context used for admission;
- =ReservationIdentity=: the budget reservation assigned to the execution;
- =OutcomeIdentity=: the single terminal outcome;
- =SuccessorContextIdentity=: the context returned after reconciliation.

Retries and resumptions reuse semantic lineage, not execution identity.

Two physical deliveries with the same =DemandExecutionIdentity= are duplicate
deliveries of one logical execution.  They cannot create two terminal outcomes.

* Outcome algebra

** ResultOutcome

=ResultOutcome= means the evaluation completed normally after a
=Continue= control decision.

It carries:

- the POO-native result value or result reference;
- result provenance and observation boundary;
- the authorizing =Continue= decision receipt;
- the successor context identity;
- reconciliation, cleanup, and cache receipts;
- publication qualification or explicit non-publication evidence.

=Continue= authorizes normal completion.  It is not itself the result value.

** ConditionOutcome

=ConditionOutcome= means a typed =ControlCondition= crossed the evaluator
boundary without being resumed inside that evaluation.

It carries:

- the typed condition and its provenance;
- whether the condition was raised continuably;
- the terminal control decision applied at the boundary;
- any process-local resumption capability reference;
- any portable suspension descriptor;
- the successor context and terminal receipts.

A terminal =ConditionOutcome= must be paired with =Suspend=, =Deny=, or
=Cancel=.  It cannot be paired with =Continue=.

When a continuable condition is authorized to continue and its handler resumes
inside the same evaluation, that condition is an intermediate observation.  It
does not become the terminal outcome.  The evaluation later produces exactly
one terminal outcome.

** SuspendedOutcome

=SuspendedOutcome= means the current local path has stopped under an authorized
=Suspend= decision.

It carries:

- a pure portable suspension descriptor;
- the originating condition or suspension reason;
- a process-local continuation reference only when one remains live;
- continuation fence, one-shot policy, and expiry evidence;
- all terminal reconciliation and cleanup receipts.

The portable descriptor is non-callable and contains no live capability.

** DeniedOutcome

=DeniedOutcome= means policy refused the requested evaluation or recovery.

It carries:

- the typed denial reason;
- the decisive policy and authority evidence;
- redacted diagnostic evidence;
- all terminal reconciliation and cleanup receipts.

Denial performs no result publication and grants no continuation authority.

** CancelledOutcome

=CancelledOutcome= means an authorized cancellation terminated the current
local path.

It carries:

- the typed cancellation reason;
- the authorizing capability and decision receipts;
- cleanup completion evidence;
- all terminal reconciliation receipts.

Cancellation cannot be represented as an ordinary returned value.

* Resume is not an outcome

=Resume= is deliberately absent from the outcome algebra.

Resume is a coordinator operation:

1. inspect a prior =ConditionOutcome= or =SuspendedOutcome=;
2. verify a fresh principal, policy, capability, fence, and budget admission;
3. create a new =DemandExecutionIdentity= and a new evaluation context;
4. link the new demand to the prior outcome with a =resumes= relation;
5. evaluate the new demand and produce its own terminal outcome.

A local evaluator that returns =Resume= violates this contract and fails
closed.

* Context transition contract

The successor context is required on every terminal path, including failure,
denial, suspension, and cancellation.

The successor context records:

- consumed and returned budget;
- child-demand reconciliation;
- redaction and retention narrowing;
- cache qualification or rejection;
- completed cleanup obligations;
- continuation lifecycle changes;
- terminal observation provenance.

The successor context cannot:

- widen authority;
- weaken redaction or retention;
- erase historical cost;
- import a simulation cache into an admitted cache;
- restore an expired or consumed continuation;
- change the identity of its input context.

* Exactly-once reconciliation

Exactly-once is a logical receipt property.  It does not claim that machines,
messages, or effectful operations execute physically exactly once.

For one =DemandExecutionIdentity=:

- exactly one reservation is admitted;
- at most one terminal outcome identity may be bound;
- terminalization requires exactly one reconciliation receipt;
- every reserved unit is classified as consumed, returned, or explicitly
  unresolved by a typed terminal fault;
- every cleanup obligation is discharged or represented by a typed cleanup
  failure;
- every cache effect is published, retained privately, rejected, or
  invalidated by explicit receipt.

The terminal outcome is not visible as complete until reconciliation and
cleanup evidence are attached.

** Idempotent terminalization

Terminalization uses a stable idempotency identity derived from:

- demand execution identity;
- reservation identity;
- candidate outcome identity;
- input context identity;
- evaluator and policy versions.

Replaying an identical terminalization returns the already-bound outcome and
successor context.  It does not consume budget, publish cache data, or run
cleanup a second time.

A replay that presents a different candidate outcome, reservation, input
context, or version under the same execution identity is a conflict and fails
closed.

** Parent and child reconciliation

Every child demand owns a child reservation and produces a child
reconciliation receipt.

The parent incorporates each child receipt once.  A duplicate child receipt is
idempotent only when its content identity is identical.  A conflicting receipt
is a typed reconciliation fault.

Historical child cost never decreases when the parent creates its successor
context.

* Receipt chain

The terminal receipt chain is:

#+begin_example
AdmissionReceipt
  -> BudgetReservation
  -> EvaluationEvidence
  -> ControlDecisionReceipt
  -> CleanupReceipt
  -> CacheEffectReceipt
  -> ReconciliationReceipt
  -> EvaluationOutcome
  -> SuccessorContextReceipt
#+end_example

Each arrow denotes a content-identified dependency.  Later receipts cannot
rewrite earlier receipts.

The chain may record explicit absence, such as =no cache publication= or
=no live continuation=.  Absence must not be inferred from a missing receipt.

* Budget behavior

Budget accounting includes:

- successful evaluation work;
- child evaluation work;
- failed attempts;
- condition construction and handling;
- suspension capture;
- cleanup;
- redaction;
- cache qualification;
- reconciliation itself.

Suspension, denial, cancellation, and typed failure do not erase work already
performed.

Unused reservation may return to the successor ledger only through the
reconciliation receipt.  No evaluator mutates a shared budget counter.

* Cleanup behavior

Cleanup obligations are registered before effectful entry and are discharged
through =dynamic-wind= compatible boundaries.

Capturing or invoking a continuation does not bypass cleanup accounting.

For one cleanup obligation:

- the same successful discharge is observed once;
- an identical replay returns the existing receipt;
- a conflicting second discharge fails closed;
- cleanup failure becomes typed terminal evidence;
- terminalization cannot silently omit the obligation.

* Cache and publication behavior

Only a qualified =ResultOutcome= from an admitted, non-simulation context may
publish into an admitted read-cache namespace.

=ConditionOutcome=, =SuspendedOutcome=, =DeniedOutcome=, and
=CancelledOutcome= do not publish result values into that namespace.

They may produce:

- diagnostic cache evidence within their authorized retention boundary;
- invalidation evidence;
- explicit non-publication evidence.

Simulation caches remain private to the simulation lineage.  No terminal
outcome promotes simulation entries into an admitted cache.

* Continuation behavior

A live continuation:

- is process-local;
- is opaque;
- is one-shot by default;
- is bound to its principal, policy, context, and continuation fence;
- is never serialized into the portable descriptor;
- is consumed, expired, or retained by explicit lifecycle receipt.

Invoking a continuation is not terminal reconciliation.  The resumed execution
must still reach one terminal outcome, or the coordinator must terminalize it
with an authorized control effect.

* Failure behavior

The following conditions fail closed with typed evidence:

- a second non-identical terminal outcome for one execution identity;
- missing or stale reservation identity;
- double consumption or double return;
- missing child reconciliation;
- incomplete cleanup evidence;
- unqualified admitted-cache publication;
- simulation-cache promotion;
- a terminal condition paired with =Continue=;
- a local evaluator returning =Resume=;
- successor context authority widening;
- resumption without fresh coordinator admission.

Failure to close a receipt chain is not reported as a completed outcome.

* Public interface boundary

The ordinary user composition surface does not expose receipt construction.

The public advanced extension surface uses native =gerbil-poo= objects and
prototype operations for:

- outcome specialization;
- typed condition specialization;
- receipt inspection and explanation;
- policy-constrained evaluator extension.

No public macro is introduced by this RFC.  Any future hygienic macro must be a
small RFC-reviewed projection that expands to these POO-native values and pure
functions.

* Successor authority and coordinator admission closure

** Requirement

A context transition is not sufficient evidence for resumption.  The
successor evaluation MUST bind the following facts in one closure:

- authority identity is preserved and authority generation strictly advances;
- successor capabilities are a subset of predecessor capabilities;
- the coordinator admission identifies the exact predecessor and successor
  contexts;
- predecessor and successor execution identities are distinct;
- the admission epoch strictly advances beyond the previously consumed
  admission epoch;
- the admission is issued by the expected coordinator;
- the verifier-owned checkpoint watermark names the same publication domain
  and authority and does not exceed the successor generation.

The successor context may refresh policy, authorization, and revocation
snapshots, but freshness of those identities does not authorize capability
widening and does not make a reused coordinator admission fresh.

** Observation and countermodels

The existing context freshness, monotonicity, and checkpoint theorems are
individually sound but do not establish the combined resumption boundary.
Two countermodels remain possible if they are consumed independently:

1. authority identity is unchanged and generation increases, while the
   successor capability set gains a new capability;
2. context transition and capability narrowing are valid, while the
   coordinator admission reuses the previously consumed epoch.

Therefore a fresh-looking successor context is not itself an admission
receipt.  Local success in each evidence plane does not imply cross-plane
binding.

** Mathematical contract

#+begin_src typst :file ../../../.data/45-107-successor-admission-binding.svg
$ "ContextTransition"(b, a) :=
    b."authority" = a."authority" and b."generation" < a."generation" $

$ "NoWiden"(b, a) := "Capabilities"(a) subset.eq "Capabilities"(b) $

$ "ExactAdmission"(r, b, a) :=
    r."before" = b and r."after" = a $

$ "FreshAdmission"(e, r) := e < r."epoch" $

$ "NewExecution"(r) :=
    r."predecessor-execution" != r."successor-execution" $

$ "CheckpointBound"(w, r, a) :=
    w."domain" = r."domain" and
    w."authority" = a."authority" and
    w."minimum-generation" <= a."generation" $

$ "SuccessorClosed"(b, a, e, r, w) :=
    "ContextTransition"(b, a) and
    "NoWiden"(b, a) and
    "ExactAdmission"(r, b, a) and
    "FreshAdmission"(e, r) and
    "NewExecution"(r) and
    "CheckpointBound"(w, r, a) $

$ "ContextTransition"(b, a) not=> "NoWiden"(b, a) $

$ "ContextTransition"(b, a) and "NoWiden"(b, a)
    not=> "FreshAdmission"(e, r) $
#+end_src

** Decision

- =ReceiptValidationContext= remains the identity/snapshot context; capability
  authority is modeled by an explicit authority envelope rather than silently
  added to receipt identity.
- Coordinator admission is a first-class receipt with coordinator identity,
  monotonically advancing admission epoch, distinct predecessor/successor
  execution identities, exact predecessor/successor contexts, and
  publication-domain identity.
- =ReceiptContextSuccessorAdmissionBindingClosure= is the bridge owner.  It
  consumes the existing freshness, monotonicity, and checkpoint owners and
  does not redefine them.
- Resumption is admitted only by the combined closure.  No individual
  freshness, generation, capability, or checkpoint proof may substitute for
  it.
