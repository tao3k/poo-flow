#+TITLE: ASCENT package boundary for POO Flow and MRR
#+DATE: 2026-09-26

The Scheme ASCENT kernel and its implementation qualification live in the
[[https://github.com/tao3k/gerbil-ascent][gerbil-ascent]] package. POO Flow includes that repository as the pinned
=packages/gerbil-ascent= submodule and runs
=t/qualification/ascent-integration/guarded-test.ss= with its Observability
Case extension. Foundation is the leaf package's sole direct dependency; it
supplies the POO and ASP packages. POO Flow does not duplicate its evaluator or differential
oracle.

MRR owns the semantic Ascent adapter, source identity, lineage, and admission.
Its CI checks those MRR contracts without installing the Scheme package.
The leaf package's =docs/research-program.org= holds the detailed rule
experiments, performance receipts, Rust Ascent comparison, and open research
questions.
