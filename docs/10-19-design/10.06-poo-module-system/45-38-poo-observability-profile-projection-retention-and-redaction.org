#+title: POO Observability Profile, Projection, Retention and Redaction
#+status: Accepted
#+language: en

* Decision

An observability policy is an ordinary POO Profile that controls authorized
projection, retention, redaction and export permission for semantic evidence.

It does not control whether correctness evidence exists and it cannot alter:

- canonical business values;
- native POO composition or prototype precedence;
- slot-contract and priority decisions;
- dependency topology;
- fixed-point convergence;
- canonical business semantic receipts;
- evidence-kernel receipts.

The system root may link a distinct observability-policy and projection
receipt.  That receipt does not become part of the canonical business-value
identity.

* Ordinary Profile composition

Observability policy participates through the established Profile interface:

#+begin_src scheme
(user-composition my-system
  (compose profiles
    wendao-episteme
    production
    production-observability))
#+end_src

=production-observability= is a native POO Profile.  It is not:

- a new configuration language;
- a provider;
- a nested =user-composition=;
- a second =compose= layer;
- an alist of logging options;
- a command-style cache-debug interface.

The Profile expands to ordinary native POO objects, contracts and policy
composition.

* Four-layer separation

#+begin_src mermaid
flowchart LR
  B["Canonical business evaluation"] -->|"always produces the correctness floor"| K["Semantic evidence kernel"]
  P["Observability POO Profile"] -->|"selects projection, retention and redaction policy"| D["Projection decision"]
  A["Current viewer authority"] -->|"attenuates the Profile ceiling"| D
  K -->|"supplies immutable semantic commitments"| D
  D -->|"does not create business demand"| V["Authorized evidence view"]
  V -->|"selects Org, Mermaid or interactive rendering"| R["Renderer adapter"]
  V -->|"external publication becomes an inert effect intent"| E["Runtime export boundary"]
#+end_src

The layers are:

1. semantic evidence kernel;
2. observability policy;
3. authorized evidence projection;
4. operational export and rendering.

No layer may silently absorb another.

* Correctness evidence floor

The evidence kernel always retains the semantic commitments required to
justify:

- contribution closure;
- prototype and slot-definition identity;
- slot-contract and priority selection;
- capability and authority decisions;
- dependency footprint;
- SCC topology and convergence;
- invalidation and reuse;
- continuable conditions and generation transitions;
- predecessor receipt chains;
- deterministic replay.

An observability Profile cannot disable, weaken or redact the kernel itself.
It controls an authorized view over the kernel.

Optional diagnostic details may have separate retention policy, but the causal
and identity commitments required by an active proof remain available.

* Receipt separation

| Receipt | Effect of changing only observability policy |
|---------+----------------------------------------------|
| Canonical business semantic receipt | Unchanged |
| Semantic evidence-kernel receipt | Unchanged |
| Observability-policy receipt | Changed when policy semantics change |
| Authorized projection receipt | Changed when viewer authority, redaction or projection changes |
| Operational export receipt | Changed with renderer, destination, host and execution behavior |

The composed system root may link all applicable receipts.  Linking does not
collapse their content identities.

#+begin_src mermaid
flowchart TD
  S["Composed system root"] -->|"commits business composition separately"| B["Business semantic receipt"]
  S -->|"commits correctness evidence separately"| K["Evidence-kernel receipt"]
  S -->|"links the selected Profile policy"| P["Observability-policy receipt"]
  P -->|"combines with current viewer authority"| V["Projection receipt"]
  V -->|"may be rendered or exported operationally"| O["Operational receipt"]
  B -->|"remains stable across view-only policy changes"| C["Canonical business identity"]
  K -->|"remains stable across view-only policy changes"| C
#+end_src

* POO policy algebra

Policy dimensions are POO contract objects with explicit algebraic laws.  They
are not raw =minimal=, =debug= or =full= enum switches.

| Policy dimension | Required composition law |
|------------------+--------------------------|
| Visibility authority | Attenuation; composition cannot broaden authority |
| Projection detail | Requested detail is bounded by Profile ceiling and viewer authority |
| Correctness retention floor | Maximum of all active semantic lower bounds |
| Requested retention floor | Maximum of authorized operator lower bounds |
| Security retention ceiling | Minimum of all applicable security upper bounds |
| Resource retention ceiling | Minimum of all admitted storage and lifecycle upper bounds |
| Redaction | Monotone protection; composition cannot accidentally reveal more |
| Export permission | Intersection of allowed semantic scope, destination class and current authority |
| Projection budget | Delegated and conserved across submodules |

Conflicting policy objects produce explicit ambiguity or contract failure.  The
system does not use Profile order or worker timing as a hidden tiebreaker.

* Retention interval

Retention is a constrained interval rather than one duration number.

#+begin_example
effective retention lower bound
= max(correctness floors,
      active causal-chain requirements,
      authorized requested floors)

effective retention upper bound
= min(security ceilings,
      privacy ceilings,
      admitted resource ceilings)
#+end_example

The interval is valid only when the lower bound does not exceed the upper
bound.

#+begin_src mermaid
flowchart TD
  C["Correctness retention floor"] -->|"keeps active generation, continuation and replay evidence reachable"| I["Effective retention interval"]
  O["Authorized operator request"] -->|"may raise the retention floor"| I
  S["Security and privacy ceilings"] -->|"limit maximum retention"| I
  R["Resource admission ceiling"] -->|"limits materialized projection cost"| I
  I -->|"lower bound is within upper bound"| P["Valid POO retention policy"]
  I -->|"lower bound exceeds upper bound"| X["Explicit policy conflict"]
#+end_src

A policy conflict cannot be resolved by deleting correctness evidence or
silently ignoring a security ceiling.

* Evidence lifecycle classes

Evidence retention distinguishes:

- active correctness evidence required by the current generation;
- predecessor evidence required by a live causal or resumption chain;
- durable canonical receipts required for replay;
- optional expanded diagnostic projections;
- disposable renderer output;
- operational telemetry.

Evicting optional projections or renderer output does not delete the semantic
receipt they project.

* Redaction preserves causality

Redaction hides values, not causal structure.

An authorized projection may report:

#+begin_example
phi+1 input <redacted-identity> changed
-> slot wendao.index invalidated
-> production.summary recomputed
#+end_example

It MUST NOT reduce this to:

#+begin_example
production.summary recomputed for an unknown reason
#+end_example

The redacted projection retains:

- a stable authorized identity commitment;
- the semantic relation to predecessor and successor values;
- demand-propagation edges;
- generation and SCC identity;
- the reason for redaction;
- the authority required for a less-redacted projection.

No projection contains live capability or authority handles.

* Viewer authority

The effective view is bounded by both Profile policy and the authority of the
current viewer.

#+begin_example
effective projection authority
= attenuation(profile visibility ceiling,
              current viewer authority,
              evidence subject restrictions)
#+end_example

Possessing a system object or evidence receipt does not automatically grant
access to every projection.

A new viewer projection has its own receipt.  It does not mutate the evidence
kernel or a projection previously issued to another viewer.

* Projection is demand-free

Projection reads already-observed semantic commitments.  It MUST NOT force an
unobserved business slot to obtain more detail.

#+begin_src mermaid
flowchart LR
  Q["Request detailed explanation"] -->|"reads existing semantic commitments only"| P["Pure projection work"]
  P -->|"projection budget remains"| V["Detailed authorized view"]
  P -->|"projection budget is exhausted"| C["Partial view plus projection condition"]
  C -->|"may continue projection without changing business generation"| V
  Q -->|"cannot force an unobserved business slot"| N["Business dependency graph remains unchanged"]
#+end_src

Projection may expand already-retained source, binding and causal metadata.  It
cannot reconstruct missing semantic observations by evaluating the business
graph.

* Projection budgets

Large causal closures, source maps and Mermaid or Org renderings may require
bounded work.

Projection budgets are distinct from:

- business-demand budgets;
- SCC convergence budgets;
- runtime realization budgets;
- external export budgets.

Exhausting a projection budget produces a partial authorized view and, when
appropriate, a continuable projection condition.  It does not prove business
non-convergence and does not create a new business contribution generation.

* Renderer boundary

The evidence POO graph is the semantic interface.  Renderers are adapters.

An Org, Mermaid, text, JSON or interactive renderer:

- consumes an authorized projection;
- does not query hidden business slots;
- does not change evidence identity;
- does not choose semantic primary causes by output order;
- preserves links to the complete cause closure;
- records renderer-specific behavior operationally.

Org remains the durable documentation format for this project.  Mermaid is the
required graph projection where causal state is materially clearer.  Neither
format defines runtime semantics.

* External export boundary

Permission to describe an export does not perform the export.

#+begin_src mermaid
flowchart LR
  P["Observability Profile"] -->|"permits an export description within a bounded scope"| I["Inert export intent"]
  I -->|"current authority validates viewer, destination and redaction"| H["Effect-boundary handoff"]
  H -->|"runtime checks current external state"| R["Authorized export"]
  R -->|"links semantic and projection receipt identities"| O["Operational export receipt"]
  I -->|"authority, destination or policy differs"| X["Reject, refine or suspend"]
#+end_src

The Profile MUST NOT embed a side effect that uploads evidence during pure
composition.

* Submodule policy delegation

An observability Profile may delegate projection and retention budgets to
nested submodules.

Delegation:

- retains parent authority ceilings;
- preserves budget conservation;
- may further attenuate visibility;
- cannot delete evidence required by a cross-submodule SCC or causal chain;
- produces nested policy and projection receipts;
- does not alter submodule business identity.

A cross-submodule explanation remains causally complete even when individual
values are redacted under different nested policies.

* Operational consequences

More detailed projection or longer optional retention may increase operational
cost.  Such cost may affect:

- host admission;
- projection latency;
- storage allocation;
- export scheduling;
- optional projection availability.

It does not affect:

- contribution selection;
- prototype precedence;
- business slot results;
- fixed-point convergence;
- canonical business semantic identity.

Operational differences belong in operational receipts.

* Required failure conditions

Policy composition MUST fail closed or raise an authorized condition when:

- a Profile attempts to disable correctness evidence;
- an observability policy changes a business value or merge decision;
- retention lower bounds exceed security or resource ceilings;
- redaction removes a causal edge or presents hidden evidence as absent;
- viewer authority is broader than the Profile ceiling;
- a projection forces an unobserved business slot;
- projection-budget exhaustion is reported as business non-convergence;
- a renderer changes semantic identity;
- export occurs during pure composition;
- a submodule policy broadens parent authority or violates budget
  conservation;
- operational cost changes the canonical business receipt.

* Acceptance checklist

- [X] Observability policy is an ordinary native POO Profile.
- [X] Correctness evidence is mandatory and independent of Profile selection.
- [X] Business, kernel, policy, projection and operational receipts remain
  distinct.
- [X] Policy dimensions use POO contract algebra rather than raw enum switches.
- [X] Retention is bounded by explicit lower and upper constraints.
- [X] Redaction preserves causal structure and identity commitments.
- [X] Effective visibility attenuates Profile and viewer authority.
- [X] Projection never demands unobserved business slots.
- [X] Projection budgets do not change business convergence.
- [X] Renderers are adapters over authorized evidence views.
- [X] External export remains an explicit effect-boundary operation.
- [X] Submodule delegation preserves authority and budget conservation.
- [ ] Validate the policy with executable business-identity, visibility,
  retention-conflict, redaction, unobserved-slot, projection-budget,
  renderer-variation, export and nested-delegation scenarios before
  implementation begins.
