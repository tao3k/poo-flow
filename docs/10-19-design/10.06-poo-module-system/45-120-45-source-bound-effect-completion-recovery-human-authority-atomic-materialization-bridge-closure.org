* Decision

RFC 45-120-45 closes the explicit RFC 149-03 gap:

#+begin_example
Connect AdmissionReceiptValid to the concrete RFC 149-01 and RFC 149-02
proof projections.
#+end_example

The canonical bridge combines:

1. the exact RFC 45-120-44 Cedar authorization subject and distinct decision
   receipts;
2. RFC 149-02 subject-bound Human Authority, delegation, revocation, and
   separation of duties;
3. an accountable assignment for the exact materialization subject;
4. proof that the authorized responsibility scope covers the exact
   materialization plan;
5. RFC 149-03 admission and atomic commit validity against the current
   governance and runtime snapshot.

The bridge does not infer Human Authority from Cedar, does not infer
accountability from an approver name, and does not infer an atomic commit from
matching receipt fields.

This is one proof composition. No second authority model, transaction model,
or alternate admission path is introduced.

* Lean-found architecture gaps

** Cedar authorization does not prove Human Authority

An exact authorized subject can coexist with a Human Authority validity
relation that rejects its receipt.

#+begin_src typst
$ "authorization bridge closed"(A, S)
  "does not imply"
  "human authority closed"(H, S) $
#+end_src

The independent theorem =authorizationDoesNotProveHumanAuthority= proves the
counterexample. The actual bridge separately requires
=humanPromotionEvidenceClosed=.

** Distinct engine identities do not assign engine roles

RFC 45-120-44 proves that two engine identities differ. The RFC 149-03
admission receipt has specifically named Cedar and Lean receipt fields.
Distinctness alone does not establish their orientation.

#+begin_src typst
$ e_L != e_R
  "does not imply"
  "engine roles valid"(e_L, e_R) $
#+end_src

=distinctEngineIdentitiesDoNotAssignCedarAndLeanRoles= proves the gap. The
actual bridge retains
=SourceBoundEffectCompletionRecoveryDualDecisionEngineRolesValid= before
binding the left receipt to =cedarDecisionReceiptId= and the right receipt to
=leanDecisionReceiptId=.

** Human authority at admission does not prove current governance

A grant and receipt can be valid for the authorization subject while the
authority snapshot changes before materialization.

#+begin_src typst
$ "human authority closed"(H, S)
  "does not imply"
  "commit subject matches current governance"(C, G) $
#+end_src

=humanAuthorityAtAdmissionDoesNotProveCurrentGovernance= constructs this
counterexample. The actual bridge requires both:

- Human evidence bound to =C.authoritySnapshotDigest=;
- =promotionCommitEvidenceClosed= against the current =GovernanceSnapshot=.

=staleGovernanceRejectsHumanAtomicBridge= rejects the stale transaction.

** A responsibility-scope identifier does not authorize a plan

A nonempty scope digest does not prove that the requested materialization plan
is inside that scope.

#+begin_src typst
$ "responsibility scope" != emptyset
  "does not imply"
  "scope covers plan"("responsibility scope", "plan digest") $
#+end_src

=nonemptyResponsibilityScopeDoesNotCoverMaterializationPlan= proves the
missing implication. The actual bridge retains
=SourceBoundEffectCompletionRecoveryResponsibilityScopeCoversPlanValid=.

** A plan digest is not plan validity

The plan digest preserves identity. It does not prove that the Marlin plan is
well formed, admitted, or executable by the selected materialization owner.

#+begin_src typst
$ "plan digest" != emptyset
  "does not imply"
  "materialization plan valid"("plan digest") $
#+end_src

The bridge therefore keeps
=SourceBoundEffectCompletionRecoveryMaterializationPlanValid= independent.

** Matching admission fields do not prove admission authority

An admission can copy the exact subject, decision evidence identity, and Human
Authority receipt identity while its owner-validity relation is false.

#+begin_src typst
$ "matching admission fields"(a)
  "does not imply"
  "admission receipt valid"(a) $
#+end_src

The theorem =matchingAdmissionFieldsDoNotProveAdmissionAuthority= preserves
the RFC 149-03 =AdmissionReceiptValid= owner boundary.

** Matching commit fields do not prove atomic commit authority

The same defect exists at commit time:

#+begin_src typst
$ "matching commit fields"(c, q)
  "does not imply"
  "commit receipt valid"(c) $
#+end_src

=matchingCommitFieldsDoNotProveCommitAuthority= proves the counterexample.
The actual bridge reuses =CommitReceiptValid= inside
=promotionCommitEvidenceClosed=.

** Execution authority and accountability are different assignments

The lifecycle acting authority must be the transaction executor. The
accountable identity is assigned independently for the complete commit
subject.

#+begin_src typst
$ "authority identity"(L) = "executor"("actors") $

$ "accountability assignment valid"(
  "accountability identity"(L),
  "actors",
  "commit subject"(a)
) $
#+end_src

Neither equation is derived from the Cedar decision or Human approver receipt.
