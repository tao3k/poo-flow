#+title: Coordinator Admission, Effect Receipt, and Budget Settlement
#+author: POO Flow
#+language: en

* Status

Accepted.

* Context

A pure =RecoveryDecision= cannot authorize itself, consume budget, or execute a
runtime effect.  The coordinator must validate current evidence and authority
before one bounded attempt.

The admission transaction can be made atomic with respect to the authoritative
evidence store, decision identity, capability validation, and budget
reservation.  An arbitrary external effect cannot generally be made
exactly-once.  POO Flow therefore separates coordinator admission evidence from
effect outcome evidence and fails closed when the outcome is indeterminate.

* Decision

The coordinator produces two distinct authoritative POO-native receipts:

- =RecoveryDecisionReceipt= records admission;
- =EffectReceipt= records the execution outcome.

The semantic guarantee is:

#+begin_quote
Exactly-once admission, receipt-backed idempotency, and fail-closed handling of
indeterminate external effects.
#+end_quote

POO Flow does not claim exactly-once execution for arbitrary external effects.

* Recovery decision receipt

=RecoveryDecisionReceipt= is authoritative semantic evidence for one admission
attempt.  It MUST identify:

- the stable decision identity;
- the authoritative evidence basis and generation;
- the profile-selected policy provenance;
- the capability and rooted delegation evidence checked by the coordinator;
- the typed recovery-action budget request;
- the reservation or rejection result;
- the coordinator identity and validation provenance;
- the admission outcome.

Admission outcomes are:

- accepted;
- rejected.

An accepted receipt permits one bounded execution attempt.  It is not itself an
effect result.

A rejected receipt MUST NOT:

- resolve a runtime handle;
- invoke the recovery handler;
- consume the requested recovery-action budget;
- imply that the underlying observation was repaired.

Validation costs belong to the separately governed closure-evaluation budget.

* Effect receipt

=EffectReceipt= is authoritative semantic evidence for the admitted execution
attempt.  It MUST distinguish:

- =succeeded=;
- =failed-before-effect=;
- =indeterminate=.

=failed-before-effect= requires evidence that the external effect did not
begin.  A generic exception is not sufficient proof.

=indeterminate= means the coordinator cannot prove whether the external effect
occurred or completed.  It MUST fail closed and MUST NOT be converted into a
safe retry assumption.

The receipt MUST identify the decision receipt, bounded attempt identity,
runtime handler identity, outcome evidence, and any provider receipt required
by the effect Contract.

* Budget reservation and settlement

The admission transaction reserves the typed recovery-action budget before the
runtime handler is invoked.

Settlement follows authoritative outcome evidence:

- a successful effect consumes the reservation;
- a provable =failed-before-effect= outcome may release the reservation when
  permitted by the budget Contract;
- an indeterminate outcome consumes or quarantines the reservation;
- a rejected admission creates no recovery-action reservation.

Quarantined budget is unavailable for reuse until a separately authorized
reconciliation produces authoritative evidence.  Telemetry, logs, or operator
belief cannot release it.

The same decision identity MUST NOT reserve or consume the same typed budget
twice.

* Idempotency

The decision identity is the coordinator idempotency key.

When a receipt already exists, a repeated submission MUST:

- return or project the existing authoritative result;
- avoid a new budget reservation;
- avoid runtime-handle resolution;
- avoid handler execution;
- avoid an unrelated successor generation.

An external provider MAY support a stronger provider-native idempotency key.
That evidence refines the =EffectReceipt= but does not weaken coordinator
admission rules.

* Successor evidence

Every accepted, rejected, failed-before-effect, succeeded, or indeterminate
path produces immutable successor evidence.

The successor =ObservationEvent= MUST causally reference:

- the originating observation evidence;
- the =RecoveryDecision=;
- the =RecoveryDecisionReceipt=;
- the =EffectReceipt= when execution was admitted;
- the typed budget settlement evidence.

The originating observation, decision, and receipts remain immutable.

* Runtime-handle boundary

A runtime-only handle, including a continuation handle, may be resolved only
after an accepted =RecoveryDecisionReceipt= exists.

The handle MUST NOT appear in:

- =RecoveryDecision=;
- either canonical receipt projection;
- =ObservationEvent=;
- telemetry;
- cache identity;
- serialization.

The coordinator records handle-resolution outcome evidence without exposing the
handle itself.

* Conformance checklist

- [X] Admission evidence and effect outcome evidence are separate receipts.
- [X] Admission is idempotent by stable decision identity.
- [X] Accepted admission reserves typed recovery-action budget.
- [X] Rejected admission invokes no handler and reserves no recovery-action
  budget.
- [X] Effect outcomes distinguish success, failure before effect, and
  indeterminate outcome.
- [X] Indeterminate outcomes fail closed.
- [X] Budget settlement follows authoritative outcome evidence.
- [X] Existing receipts prevent duplicate execution and double spending.
- [X] Runtime handles resolve only after accepted admission.
- [X] Successor events retain the complete causal receipt chain.
- [ ] Concrete coordinator and receipt source owners have been resolved through
  parser-owned owner discovery.
- [ ] Implementation has been admitted after owner resolution.

* Non-goals

- Claiming exactly-once execution for arbitrary external effects.
- Treating a generic exception as proof that no effect occurred.
- Releasing quarantined budget from telemetry or operator belief.
- Serializing runtime handles.
- Combining admission and external execution into an unobservable command.
- Defining an implementation schedule.
