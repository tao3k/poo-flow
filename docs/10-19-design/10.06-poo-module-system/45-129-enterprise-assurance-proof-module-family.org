* Purpose

Enterprise assurance is a core POO Flow product surface.  It SHALL be
maintained as an independent, highly modular Lean interface rather than as one
large contributor-governance proof file.

This RFC refines the scenario and contributor architecture in
=45-128-real-world-ai-assurance-scenarios-and-adapter-governance.org=.

The enterprise proof family connects contributor qualification to existing
generic POO Flow proofs for governance decisions, capability attenuation,
principal/key lifecycle, federation, fixed-point composition, authorization,
and runtime evidence.  It does not duplicate those owners.

* Stable aggregate interface

Downstream proofs SHALL import:

#+begin_src lean
import PooFlowProof.PooC3.Enterprise
#+end_src

The aggregate module currently re-exports the Enterprise assurance surface.
Internal owners remain independently buildable and reviewable.

| =Enterprise.lean= | Stable aggregate import; no new semantics |

The previous exploratory single owner
=ContributorEnterpriseGovernance.lean= is removed.  It is not part of the
public proof surface.

* Owner boundaries

** Generic proof owners

- =GovernanceDecisionAuthority= owns generic governance decision states,
  quorum evidence, separation of duties, one-shot scoped capabilities,
  revocation, and break-glass constraints.
- =PrincipalKeyLifecycle= owns key binding, purpose/scope checks, rotation,
  revocation, historical verification, and algorithm migration.
- =CrossDomainFederation= owns domain preservation, capability attenuation,
  disclosure projection, responsibility allocation, federation revocation,
  and transfer authority.
- =ContributorRepositoryQualification= owns open facets, contributor asset
  qualification, explicit authorization evidence, responsibility evidence,
  and repository non-authority.

** Enterprise proof owners

Enterprise modules own only the refinement that requires these generic
obligations together for enterprise admission.  They do not redefine Cedar
authorization, POO composition, contributor semantics, keys, federation, or
generic governance decisions.

** Domain and organization owners

Enterprise organization structures, job titles, approval thresholds,
jurisdictions, industry rules, and responsibility vocabularies remain
policy/profile data.  The Lean core quantifies over their identities and
relations.  It MUST NOT hard-code one corporation or one industry ontology.

* High-level admission contract

An Enterprise assurance claim is fail closed.  Missing any high-level claim
blocks =Enterprise.Assurance.Admitted=:

- governed admission;
- governance checks;
- quorum;
- separation of duties;
- evidence freshness;
- delegation attenuation;
- responsibility/accountability;
- responsibility transfer safety.
- tenant and organization isolation.

An expired or revoked governance decision cannot enter
=Enterprise.Admission.Admitted=.  Stale policy/source revisions, stale
revocation observations, or revoked evidence cannot satisfy
=Enterprise.EvidenceFreshness.Hold=.

* Why modularity is part of correctness

The split is not merely source organization.

- Each semantic family has one proof owner.
- Dependency direction prevents enterprise assumptions from contaminating
  generic POO theorems.
- Focused builds expose owner-specific failures.
- Reviewers can audit quorum, freshness, delegation, or responsibility
  transfer without accepting an unrelated monolith.
- Downstream projects receive a small stable aggregate import while internal
  theorem families can evolve independently.
- AXLE and executable evidence can issue per-owner receipts rather than one
  opaque “enterprise proof passed” claim.

* Deliberately open enterprise modules

The current module family exposes, rather than hides, the following remaining
owners:

- hierarchical and multi-parent tenant/organization topology beyond the
  initial open relation and isolation core;
- configurable N-of-M and multi-stage quorum policy;
- policy-specific role incompatibility beyond the minimal proposer,
  approver, and executor separation;
- delegated authority chains across organizations;
- evidence custody, retention, redaction, and legal hold;
- incident commander, revocation owner, remediation owner, and disclosure
  owner responsibilities;
- temporal and jurisdiction-specific policy observations;
- human/agent mixed approval and accountability;
- CedarAdapter and runtime receipt refinement.

These SHOULD become independent Enterprise modules when their contracts are
confirmed.  They MUST NOT be appended indiscriminately to =Admission.lean=.
