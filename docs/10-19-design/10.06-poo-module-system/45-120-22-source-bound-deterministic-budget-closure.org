#+title: Source-bound deterministic budget closure
#+startup: overview

* Status

Proof-backed declaration-closure v1 refinement.

This document stabilizes =poo-flow.lean-declaration-closure.v1=.  It does not
authorize a v2 schema, a second closure identity, or a competing proof path.

* Context

=SourceBoundProgressReceipt= previously carried only
=semanticBudgetRemaining : Nat= and =schedulingBudgetRemaining : Nat=.
=ProgressClass.budgetExhausted= was supported when either number was zero.

That establishes a scalar condition but not deterministic budget ownership.  A
zero value alone does not identify:

- the budget plan;
- the owner that authorized it;
- the fixed-point receipt and maximum generation covered by the plan;
- the original semantic and scheduling limits;
- the consumed amounts;
- the conservation equation;
- the cycle, continuation, and progress receipt that consumed it.

=SourceBoundFixedPointReceipt= also has no budget fields.  Therefore neither the
fixed-point receipt nor the progress receipt determines the missing budget
identity.

* Decision

Deterministic budget evidence is an independent source-bound owner composed
with progress evidence.  It has two objects:

1. =SourceBoundDeterministicBudgetPlan= authorizes semantic and scheduling
   limits for one fixed-point receipt and maximum generation.
2. =SourceBoundDeterministicBudgetReceipt= records consumed and remaining values
   for one cycle continuation and progress receipt.

The closure MUST bind:

- plan identity, owner identity, provenance, registry, fixed-point receipt, and
  maximum generation;
- receipt identity, plan identity, registry, fixed-point receipt, generation,
  cycle observation, continuation, progress receipt, and provenance;
- semantic and scheduling conservation equations;
- progress remaining values to receipt remaining values;
- =ProgressClass.budgetExhausted= to a typed zero-remaining witness.

The closure MUST compose with =SourceBoundProgressEvidenceClosed=.  Correct
arithmetic over an arbitrary or unclosed progress receipt is insufficient.

* Mathematical model

For each deterministic budget kind
=k in {semantic, scheduling}=, the authorized limit is conserved:

#+begin_src typst
$ c_k + r_k = L_k quad
  (k in { "semantic", "scheduling" }) $
#+end_src

Here =c_k= is consumed, =r_k= is remaining, and =L_k= is the plan limit.
Exhaustion is a typed witness:

#+begin_src typst
$ "BudgetExhausted" equiv exists k: r_k = 0 $
#+end_src

A runtime threshold crossing is not a deterministic-budget proof:

#+begin_src typst
$ "WatchdogExceeded" ⊬ "BudgetExhausted" $
#+end_src

The two facts may coexist, but neither substitutes for the other.

* State and authority separation

#+begin_src mermaid
stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> PlanAuthorized: owner binds limits and fixed-point receipt
  PlanAuthorized --> ConsumptionRecorded: plan identity and coordinates match
  ConsumptionRecorded --> AccountingClosed: consumed plus remaining equals limit
  AccountingClosed --> SemanticExhausted: semantic remaining equals zero
  AccountingClosed --> SchedulingExhausted: scheduling remaining equals zero
  AccountingClosed --> BudgetAvailable: both remaining values are positive
  SemanticExhausted --> BudgetExhaustedReceipt: typed semantic witness
  SchedulingExhausted --> BudgetExhaustedReceipt: typed scheduling witness
  WatchdogObserved --> RuntimeThresholdExceeded: runtime metric exceeds threshold
  RuntimeThresholdExceeded --> FailClosed: attempted budget inference
  BudgetExhaustedReceipt --> RetryAwaitingAuthority: exhaustion is not retry authority
#+end_src

* Ownership boundaries

| Owner | Owns | Does not own |
|-------+------+--------------|
| Fixed-point closure | registry, roots, visited/pending subjects, generation, stability | deterministic budget authorization |
| Progress closure | source-bound classification and subject evidence | budget plan identity or conservation |
| Deterministic-budget closure | plan identity, limits, consumption, remaining values, typed exhaustion | runtime threshold interpretation |
| Runtime-watchdog closure | runtime owner, phase, metric, unit, threshold, attribution | semantic or scheduling exhaustion |
| Recovery policy | retry or continuation authority | manufacturing evidence for any other owner |
