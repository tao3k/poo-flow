* Owner gap

Precision and recall describe detector behavior. They do not report how much
execution entered the formally provable region, how often missing evidence
caused escalation, or whether historical decisions replay against their
original bundle.

* Decision

Define one versioned =AgentVerificationSample= per action decision:

- action, decision, and evidence-root identities;
- independent bundle digest;
- proof disposition: =proved=, =unknownEscalated=, or
  =rejectedFailClosed=;
- replay disposition: =notAttempted=, =succeeded=, or =failed=;
- provenance.

Counts are derived recursively from the sample list. They are not accepted as
independent input.

* Metrics

#+begin_src typst
$ "proof coverage" =
  ("proved actions", "total actions") $

$ "unknown escalation rate" =
  ("unknown and escalated actions", "total actions") $

$ "replay success" =
  ("successful replays", "attempted replays") $
#+end_src

The Lean representation is =AgentMetricFraction= with explicit numerator and
denominator. Formatting as percentages belongs outside the proof model.

* Sample validity

=AgentVerificationMetricsClosed= requires:

- non-empty sample population;
- non-empty receipt, schema, evidence-root, bundle, policy, and provenance
  identities;
- every sample is bound to the receipt bundle;
- every proved sample satisfies an external proof-validity predicate;
- every unknown/escalated sample satisfies an escalation-validity predicate;
- every successful replay satisfies an independent replay-validity predicate;
- receipt population equals =verificationPopulation samples=.

Thus a label does not validate itself.

* Theorems

Lean proves:

- proved count is no larger than total;
- unknown/escalated count is no larger than total;
- replay attempts are no larger than total;
- replay successes are no larger than attempts;
- a closed receipt reports the three exact derived fractions;
- an unknown/escalated sample is not a proved sample;
- a sample bound to another bundle rejects the metrics closure.

* Architecture boundary

- No floating-point or rounding policy enters Lean.
- Precision/recall remain detector metrics and may be reported alongside these
  proof metrics.
- Unknown is not silently counted as safe.
- Replay success requires the independent replay-validity predicate.
- Counts do not authorize actions or recovery.
