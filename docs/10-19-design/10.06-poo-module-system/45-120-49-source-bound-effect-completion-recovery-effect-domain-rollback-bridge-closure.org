#+title: RFC 45-120-49 — Source-Bound Effect Completion Recovery Effect-Domain Rollback Bridge Closure
#+startup: overview
#+options: toc:3 num:t

* Status

Accepted.

This RFC defines one normative contract for binding authorized transaction
recovery to complete effect-domain rollback evidence.

* Ownership

- RFC47 owns authorized transaction recovery, lifecycle admission, durable
  transaction state, the exclusive recovery claim, and the recovery receipt.
- RFC149-05, implemented by
  =PooFlowProof.Enterprise.EffectDomainCoverage=, is the sole owner of effect
  universe coverage, effect-world validity, rollback obligations, compensation
  receipts, and whole-domain restoration.
- This RFC owns only the cross-domain bindings between those two owners.
- =gerbil-poo= remains the only Scheme POO library. This proof layer defines
  propositions about the architecture; it does not introduce another POO
  implementation.

* Problem

RFC47 closes who may recover a transaction and which durable receipt terminates
that recovery. Its =EffectObservation= contains a runtime-state digest, one
external-effect digest, and an uncompensated-application count. Those aggregate
values do not identify every effect in a materialization plan and cannot prove
that the complete effect world was restored.

RFC149-05 closes whole-domain rollback, but its witness and worlds are not
intrinsically connected to RFC47's request, recovery claim, observations,
fence, or owner commitment.

Therefore both proofs may be independently valid while describing different
effect universes, different worlds, or a compensation run under a stale fence.

* Normative semantic boundary

The bridge MUST require:

1. the RFC47 authorized recovery proof;
2. an effect coverage witness whose plan digest equals the request plan digest;
3. an explicit relation deriving the witness effect universe from that plan;
4. complete observed-domain coverage and functional before/after effect worlds;
5. explicit bindings from RFC47 pre/post observations to those worlds;
6. a provenance relation binding the owner commitment, recovery receipt,
   witness, obligations, compensation receipts, and worlds;
7. for a =rolledBack= outcome, compensation receipts bound to the acquired
   recovery fence and the complete RFC149-05 rollback closure.

A =committed= outcome does not create a rollback obligation. It still requires
the effect-domain coverage and observation/provenance bindings. Per-effect
exactly-once application for committed outcomes is a separate proof frontier
and MUST NOT be inferred from this RFC.

* Mathematical model

Let =p= be the materialization-plan digest, =U_p= its complete effect universe,
=D_w= the observed domain carried by witness =w=, and =W_0= and =W_1= the
before and after effect worlds.

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    B_("plan")(p, U_p)
    and U_p subset.eq D_w
    and D_w subset.eq op("dom")(W_0)
    and D_w subset.eq op("dom")(W_1)
  $
]
#+end_src

The digest equality and the derivation relation are distinct obligations:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    w_"plan" = p
    quad and quad
    B_("plan")(p, w_"universe")
  $
]
#+end_src

The first proposition identifies the request. The second establishes that the
declared universe is the universe of that request. Neither implies the other.

Let =O_0= and =O_1= be RFC47's aggregate observations and =B_obs= the external
observation-to-world relation:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    B_("observation")(O_0, W_0)
    quad and quad
    B_("observation")(O_1, W_1)
  $
]
#+end_src

For a rolled-back result, every effect in the plan universe must agree:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    q_"outcome" = "rolledBack"
    arrow.r
    forall e in U_p :
      W_0(e) = W_1(e)
  $
]
#+end_src

Compensation is valid only under the acquired recovery fence =f=:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    q_"outcome" = "rolledBack"
    arrow.r
    B_("fence")(f, C)
    and op("Closed")_(149-05)(p, U_p, W_0, W_1, C)
  $
]
#+end_src

The result-sensitive bridge is:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    op("Bridge") =
      op("Authorized")_(47)
      and op("Coverage")_(149-05)
      and B_("observation")
      and B_("provenance")
      and (
        q_"outcome" = "rolledBack"
        arrow.r
        B_("fence") and op("Closed")_(149-05)
      )
  $
]
#+end_src

For a recovery trace =T=, the obligation is pointwise over every index whose
state requires recovery:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    forall i :
      op("RecoveryRequired")(T_i)
      arrow.r
      op("Bridge")_i
  $
]
#+end_src

This quantification is what lifts the single-transaction bridge to hundreds or
thousands of independently composed modules and profiles: scale changes the
number of indexed obligations, not the meaning of an obligation.

* Countermodels found by Lean

The independent model proves the following gaps:

- Equal aggregate recovery digests can hide different effect worlds.
- A matching plan digest does not bind a declared effect universe to the plan.
- A populated observed domain does not prove complete universe coverage.
- A =rolledBack= receipt alone does not provide compensation receipts.
- Matching effect identity does not bind compensation to the recovery fence.
- A =committed= receipt does not require rollback compensation.

These are architecture defects if the corresponding bridge relations are
omitted; they are not parser or serialization concerns.

* Proof obligations

The following obligations are normative:

- A plan digest MUST NOT stand in for a plan-to-effect-universe derivation.
- An aggregate observation MUST NOT stand in for an effect world.
- A compensation receipt MUST bind the acquired recovery fence.
- The rollback path MUST reject an irreversible obligation.
- The bridge MUST preserve the RFC47 authorized recovery proof unchanged.
- The bridge MUST preserve RFC149-05 ownership of rollback semantics unchanged.
- A trace MUST provide the bridge for every recovery-required index.

* First-class trace evidence

Downstream proofs MUST consume
=SourceBoundEffectCompletionRecoveryEffectDomainRollbackTraceEvidence= rather
than repeat RFC47 and RFC49's registry and validator parameters.

The package contains the complete trace data and the existing exact trace
closure proof. Its projections are reducible definitions:

#+begin_src typst
#align(center)[
  $
    op("AuthorizedRecoveryAt")(E, i)
      = op("RFC47Bridge")(E, i) \
    op("EffectDomainBridgeAt")(E, i)
      = op("RFC49Bridge")(op("AuthorizedRecoveryAt")(E, i), E, i)
  $
]
#+end_src

Therefore packaging changes proof elaboration shape without weakening the
authorization or effect-domain proposition. The focused package build completed
in 7.4 seconds.

* Remaining frontier

This RFC closes the rolled-back result. The next independent proof question is
whether a committed result provides per-effect exactly-once application over
the same complete effect universe. That question requires a committed-effect
application owner and is intentionally not claimed here.
