#+title: Effect Intent, Suspension, Runtime Handoff, And Receipt Reinjection

* Normative responsibility

This module owns the phase boundary between pure POO composition and external
effect execution:

- pure construction of a POO effect intent;
- suspension of the demanding realization cell;
- policy-authorized runtime or Marlin handoff;
- coalescing of repeated demand onto one operation attempt;
- receipt-qualified reinjection into the suspended realization;
- projection of the effect barrier into the runtime wait graph.

It does not define a second effect evidence state machine.  Commit, failure,
cancellation, indeterminacy, and reconciliation remain owned by
[[file:45-08-effect-boundary-commit-reconciliation.org][Effect Boundary Commit And Reconciliation]].

* Ownership boundary: gerbil-poo mechanism and POO Flow governance

=gerbil-poo= owns POO identity, prototype composition, lazy demand realization,
generic suspension, memoization, lineage, and continuation-aware control.

POO Flow owns:

- the POO-native effect-intent semantic contract;
- capability and policy qualification before handoff;
- the one-demand/one-attempt coalescing rule;
- receipt validation before reinjection;
- effect-barrier evidence projected into recovery and observability.

The runtime or Marlin handoff owns actual external execution through the
qualified provider.  Macro expansion, prototype composition, normalization,
lineage inspection, and observability do not execute the effect.

* Pure phase invariant

Profile, Mix, prototype composition, macro expansion, and composition
normalization MUST remain pure.  They may construct, refine, combine, or inspect
POO values, but they MUST NOT invoke an external effect provider.

An effect intent is a POO object constructed as a pure value.  Its semantic
evidence includes:

- operation evidence;
- required provider capability;
- input provenance;
- verifier requirements;
- idempotency or reconciliation requirements when applicable;
- the realization boundary to which a future receipt must return.

Constructing or observing this object does not authorize execution.

  I -->|"suspend exactly one demand cell"| S["State: suspended realization boundary"]
  S -->|"governance qualifies capability and policy"| A["State: authorized runtime handoff"]
  A -->|"provider begins one operation attempt"| E["State: external effect execution"]
  E -->|"verifier-backed terminal evidence"| R["State: immutable effect receipt"]
  R -->|"validated receipt resumes the same demand cell"| V["State: realized POO value"]
  I -.->|"inspection and observability do not execute"| O["State: diagnostic projection"]

The diagram is a phase and handoff pipeline.  The authoritative effect evidence
transitions inside =external effect execution= and =indeterminate barrier= are
defined only by the 45-08 state machine.

* POO-native effect intent

The effect intent belongs to the ordinary POO object world.  It is not:

- a raw record or alist command DSL;
- an eagerly invoked thunk hidden in a slot;
- a macro-expansion side effect;
- a serialized continuation;
- an external runtime handle.

The public constructor and slot vocabulary remain evidence-gated until the
corresponding =gerbil-poo= surface is symbol-located and behavior-proven.  Any
eventual helper MUST expand to ordinary POO-native and functional Scheme code.

Intent refinements may add provider requirements, verification policy, or
provenance through normal prototype composition.  Refinement MUST NOT start the
operation.

* Demand suspension boundary

When realization demands an effect intent, the current demand cell suspends at
an explicit effect boundary.  The suspension preserves:

- the demand coordinate;
- the realization-context identity;
- the POO boundary lineage;
- the effect-intent evidence;
- the validated continuation authority needed for later reinjection.

The suspended continuation and demand cell remain runtime-local.  Neither is
placed in an effect receipt or cross-context evidence bundle.

Suspension alone does not mean that the provider has started.  The effect
operation remains =declared= until governance authorizes handoff and provider
invocation begins.

* Authorization and runtime handoff

Before handoff, governance validates:

- that the requested provider capability is available;
- that capability and resource policy authorize the boundary;
- that the effect intent contains sufficient operation and verifier evidence;
- that cancellation, recovery, and reconciliation requirements are supported;
- that no prior receipt already determines the same operation.

Failure of any gate raises a structured continuable condition.  The permitted
recovery actions may replace a provider, refine policy within authority, defer,
or abort the declared boundary.  They do not execute the effect implicitly.

Successful handoff establishes the provider operation and attempt evidence used
by 45-08.  The provider then advances the authoritative effect state from
=declared= to =in-flight=.

Boundary-scoped provider qualification and missing/ambiguous resolution are
specified by [[file:45-10-provider-runtime-search-closure.org][Provider Runtime Search Closure]].  This module consumes the selected,
policy-qualified POO provider capability; it does not search a global registry
or invent provider precedence.

Runtime-local resource acquisition and continuation-safe access attachment are
specified by
[[file:45-11-resource-lease-continuation-dynamic-wind.org][Resource Lease, Continuation, And Dynamic-Wind]].  This module owns handoff and
reinjection, but it does not treat =dynamic-wind= scope entry or exit as a
physical acquire or release.

Withdrawal of joined-demand authority and cancellation of a coalesced provider
attempt follow
[[file:45-12-structured-cancellation-boundary-generations.org][Structured Cancellation And Boundary Generations]].  This module preserves the
canonical suspended cell and attempt evidence; it does not use cancellation as
an implicit duplicate handoff.

Provider-capacity admission, one-permit coalesced-attempt accounting, and
backpressure follow
[[file:45-13-demand-admission-backpressure-cleanup-capacity.org][Demand Admission, Backpressure, And Cleanup Capacity]].  This module owns the
suspension and handoff boundary, not runtime scheduling.

* Repeated demand and attempt coalescing

Repeated demand with the same realization context, boundary lineage, and
provider operation evidence joins the existing suspended cell and operation
attempt.

#+begin_src mermaid
flowchart TD
  D1["State: first demand"] -->|"creates suspension boundary"| C["State: canonical suspended demand cell"]
  D2["State: repeated demand"] -->|"same context + boundary + operation"| C
  C -->|"one authorized handoff"| A["State: one active attempt"]
  A -->|"terminal receipt"| R["State: validated receipt"]
  R -->|"resume canonical cell once"| V["State: memoized realized value"]
  V -->|"all joined demands observe the same realization"| D1
  V -->|"all joined demands observe the same realization"| D2
  D2 -.->|"does not create a second invocation"| A
#+end_src

Only an immutable policy decision following a proven =failed= or =cancelled=
attempt may create a new attempt.  An =indeterminate= attempt never produces an
automatic retry.

* Receipt validation and reinjection

A terminal provider response does not directly mutate the POO object.  POO Flow
first validates the receipt against:

- the suspended context and demand coordinate;
- the boundary lineage;
- the provider operation and attempt evidence;
- the expected verifier and policy identity;
- the current cancellation and recovery authority.

A valid receipt resumes the original demand through its validated continuation
authority and associates the evidence-linked result with that demand cell.
Dependent POO demands then observe the normal realized value through
=gerbil-poo= memoization.

A stale, mismatched, duplicate, or unauthorized receipt raises a structured
condition.  It MUST NOT resume an unrelated or already-invalidated
continuation.

Continuation re-entry after =committed= observes the prior receipt and realized
value.  It never reinvokes the effect.

Replay receipt injection follows
[[file:45-14-deterministic-realization-replay.org][Deterministic Realization Replay]].  It resumes only a fresh replay demand and
never reuses the source runtime continuation or invokes a live provider.

* Fixed-point and wait-graph projection

An effect boundary is a typed wait-graph node.  It distinguishes external
progress from an ordinary POO dependency edge:

#+begin_src mermaid
flowchart LR
  P["State: POO demand node"] -->|"demands effect intent"| B["State: suspended effect-boundary node"]
  B -->|"authorized handoff waits for provider outcome"| E["State: external operation node"]
  E -->|"terminal receipt resumes demand"| P
  Q["State: dependent POO demand"] -->|"waits on realized value"| P
  E -.->|"external progress is observed, not forced by graph traversal"| W["State: observability evidence"]
  E -->|"provider waits on a descendant demand"| K["State: cross-boundary wait dependency"]
  K -->|"dependency returns to suspended boundary"| B
  K -->|"closed wait cycle with no progress authority"| X["State: structured cross-boundary deadlock evidence"]
#+end_src

A graph cycle containing an effect boundary is not automatically an ordinary
prototype cycle.  The diagnosis records separately:

- pure POO dependency edges;
- suspension and continuation edges;
- external provider wait edges;
- containment and import dependency paths;
- the authority capable of producing progress.

If the provider waits on a descendant demand that ultimately waits on the same
suspended effect boundary, POO Flow reports structured cross-boundary deadlock
evidence.  Diagnosis does not force the effect or resume the continuation.

* Observability contract

The three planes report:

| Plane | Required projection |
|---+---|
| definition | effect-intent provenance and required capability |
| realization | demand coordinate, suspension, coalescing, handoff, attempt, receipt, and reinjection |
| governance | authorization decision, policy identity, recovery choice, and immutable receipt chain |

Observability MUST distinguish intent construction from execution, suspension
from provider invocation, provider response from validated receipt, and receipt
validation from continuation resumption.

No sink may trigger handoff, retry, reconciliation, or reinjection.

* Explicit exclusions

This RFC does not authorize:

- effects during macro expansion, prototype composition, normalization, graph
  traversal, or diagnostic inspection;
- an eagerly invoked effect thunk hidden inside an ordinary slot;
- more than one active attempt for a coalesced demand;
- direct provider mutation of a POO object;
- receipt reinjection into a stale or unrelated continuation;
- serialization of a POO root, closure, continuation, demand cell, or external
  handle;
- a second effect evidence state machine outside 45-08;
- a new public effect DSL before the real =gerbil-poo= API is behavior-proven.
