#+title: Cedar and Lean dual-engine authorization subject refinement
#+startup: overview

* Status

Lean-proven required refinement of:

- =149-protocol-person-promotion-rfc.org=
- =45-135-02-resolution-and-verification-subject-binding-refinement.org=
- =45-135-03-environment-import-and-content-closure-refinement.org=

RFC 149 correctly assigns Cedar and Lean as dual decision engines and requires
their decisions to join under one Bundle identity and epoch. The current rule
does not bind the complete authorization subject and does not prove semantic
correctness.

* Cedar specification anchor

The imported Cedar specification exposes:

#+begin_src text
Cedar.Spec.isAuthorized
  (request : Cedar.Spec.Request)
  (entities : Cedar.Spec.Entities)
  (policies : Cedar.Spec.Policies)
  : Cedar.Spec.Response

Cedar.Spec.Response.decision : Cedar.Spec.Response -> Cedar.Spec.Decision
#+end_src

The POO Flow proof model defines =cedarSpecDecision= directly from this API. It
does not replace Cedar semantics with a locally invented policy decision type.

* Discovered logical defects

** Bundle and epoch do not identify an authorization subject

An authorization decision also depends on:

- principal, action, resource, and context encoded by the Cedar request;
- the policy set;
- the entity store; and
- the resolved semantics/environment used to evaluate them.

Two receipts may therefore have the same Bundle, epoch, and decision while
authorizing different requests against different policies and entities.

** Agreement does not imply correctness

Two engines can return the same wrong decision. Correlated projection errors,
stale snapshots, or a shared malformed input can make Cedar and Lean agree
without satisfying the intended policy semantics.

** Two receipts do not imply two engines

Without distinct engine and receipt identities, one valid Cedar receipt can be
joined with itself and presented as dual-engine evidence.

* Refined authorization subject

Both engine receipts must bind the same canonical subject:

#+begin_src text
AuthorizationSubject
  request_digest
  policy_set_digest
  entity_store_digest
  independent_declaration_bundle_digest
  epoch
#+end_src

The request digest must cover the complete Cedar request, including principal,
action, resource, and context. Human-readable IDs alone are not sufficient.

Policy and entity digests must identify canonical snapshots, not mutable names
or database locations.

* Refined engine receipts

#+begin_src text
DecisionReceipt
  engine_id
  engine_artifact_digest
  receipt_id
  authorization_subject
  decision
  decision_evidence_digest
  authority_signature_or_witness
#+end_src

Required engine roles are distinct:

- the Cedar receipt projects the result of =Cedar.Spec.isAuthorized= for the
  bound request, entities, and policies;
- the Lean receipt proves that the expected decision for that same subject
  refines the selected Cedar/spec semantics.

The engine validity relations are engine-specific. Field equality does not
establish Cedar execution or Lean theorem validity.

* Correct dual-engine admission rule

Promotion admission requires:

1. exact authorization subject equality;
2. distinct =engine_id= values;
3. distinct =receipt_id= values;
4. valid Cedar and Lean receipt authorities;
5. decision equality;
6. semantic correctness for the bound subject;
7. the Bundle/environment evidence closure from RFC 45-135-01 through
   45-135-03; and
8. Human Audit authority evidence bound to the same promotion subject.

Agreement is a necessary condition. It is not an oracle.

* Required rejection rules

The promotion coordinator must reject:

- request digest mismatch;
- policy set digest mismatch;
- entity store digest mismatch;
- Bundle digest or epoch mismatch;
- the same engine on both sides;
- duplicate receipt identity;
- an invalid engine authority receipt;
- decision disagreement;
- decision agreement without semantic refinement;
- a Cedar decision not derived from the bound request/entities/policies;
- a Lean theorem not bound to the same subject; and
- Human Audit authority evidence for another promotion subject.
