#+title: RFC 149-07 — Fixed-Point Closure, Termination, and Cycle-Observation Refinement
#+language: en

* Status

This is a Lean-proven refinement of RFC 149-06.  It reuses the
cycle/progress/budget/watchdog semantics of RFC 45-120-21, the relation
separation of RFC 45-120-22, and the profile-only interface boundary of
RFC 45-120-23.

The proof owner is the top-level enterprise scope:

#+begin_src text
packages/proof/lean/PooFlowProof/Enterprise/
  CompositionalFixedPointClosure.lean
#+end_src

=Enterprise= is not a child of =PooC3=.  Enterprise proofs may import
generic POO-C3 composition, policy, and authorization theorems, but the
enterprise model is owned by =PooFlowProof.Enterprise=.

This refinement defines evidence obligations.  It does not introduce a
second fixed-point runtime beside =gerbil-poo=, and it does not claim
that the current profile-composition runtime already emits these
receipts.

* Public interface remains unchanged

The user still writes:

#+begin_src scheme
(use-composition
  base-profile
  wendao-episteme-mix
  production-profile)
#+end_src

No fixed-point macro, worklist DSL, recovery form, or raw receipt alist
is added to the public Scheme surface.  =compose= remains pure;
=profiles= remain first-class POO values; =use-composition= remains the
shallow root-selection interface.

A profile may select an owner-valid progress policy and may limit
semantic, scheduling, or watchdog budgets.  It cannot grant retry
authority, own a continuation, or convert an observation into a
resumption command.

* Discovered logical defects

** A stable flag and empty pending list do not prove closure

The countermodel =fakeStableRootReceipt= contains all visible
=use-composition= roots, reports =stable=true=, and has no pending work.
It omits the nested notification workflow.

Lean proves:

#+begin_src text
stableFlagAndEmptyPendingDoNotProveLinkClosure
#+end_src

A closure receipt must independently prove that every link leaving a
visited object ends at another visited object.

** A link-closed candidate need not be the least fixed point

The set containing every possible node is closed under every link.  It
can nevertheless contain an unreachable orphan.

Lean proves:

#+begin_src text
closedCandidateNeedNotBeLeast
#+end_src

Therefore a receipt needs both directions:

- every reachable node is visited;
- every visited node is reachable.

Root inclusion plus link closure proves the first direction.  A separate
minimality witness proves the second.

** Finite discovery and scheduling are different obligations

A fresh semantic discovery adds one previously unseen registered node.
When the resulting visited list is a sub-permutation of the finite
registry, its length is bounded by the registry length.

Lean proves:

#+begin_src text
freshDiscoveryConsumesFiniteRegistryCapacity
#+end_src

This bounds semantic discovery.  It does not bound repeated dequeue,
dispatch, retry, or watchdog activity.  Those actions consume distinct
scheduling or runtime budgets.

** One scalar budget cannot identify the exhausted owner

Two observations can both report zero remaining units while one belongs
to semantic discovery and the other to scheduling dispatch.

Lean proves:

#+begin_src text
oneScalarBudgetCannotIdentifyExhaustionOwner
#+end_src

Semantic, scheduling, and runtime-watchdog budgets must have independent
owners and receipts.  Merge precedence is not a progress measure and
cannot settle any of these budgets.

** A cycle observation is evidence, not retry authority

=CycleDetectedObservation= binds:

- registry digest;
- generation;
- strongly connected component members;
- causal path;
- provenance digest;
- consumed semantic and scheduling budgets;
- continuation identity.

Lean proves:

#+begin_src text
cycleObservationDoesNotAuthorizeRetry
#+end_src

The continuation identity makes the suspension observable.  It does not
make the observation executable.  Retry still requires the existing
runtime-local, single-use =RetryCapability= and its owner-valid
admission.  A profile and a cycle receipt cannot mint that capability.

** Matching fields do not prove owner validity

Lean proves:

#+begin_src text
matchingFixedPointFieldsDoNotProveOwnerValidity
#+end_src

Digest, generation, stable state, and empty pending work remain data.
=FixedPointClosureReceiptValid=, =ProgressReceiptValid=, and
=CycleDetectedObservationValid= are independent owner relations.

* Corrected closure

The proof transport for this refinement stabilizes
=poo-flow.lean-declaration-closure.v1=.  Source ranges, generated-declaration
normalization, phase timing, and bounded root sharding are v1 implementation
refinements only.  This RFC does not authorize a v2 declaration-closure
schema, a second closure identity, or a competing proof path.

=FixedPointEvidenceClosed= requires:

- the effect witness plan and roots match the promotion request;
- the composition registry and effect witness are owner-valid;
- the fixed-point receipt is owner-valid;
- receipt registry, roots, and effect universe match the witness;
- the receipt is stable and has no pending work;
- every root is visited;
- the visited set is closed under composition links;
- every visited node is reachable from the roots;
- the progress receipt is owner-valid and binds the same registry and
  generation;
- every cycle observation is owner-valid, binds the same registry, and
  does not name a future generation.

Lean then proves:

#+begin_src text
closedReceiptContainsEveryReachableNode
closedReceiptIsLeastFixedPoint
completeFixedPointEvidenceCloses
#+end_src

The least-fixed-point theorem is the correction to the weaker
=stable=true= design.

* Mathematical model

The durable mathematical surface is Typst:

#+begin_src typst
$ N(G) = {M_"identity" | M in G} $

$ "LinkClosed"(V, G) =
  forall x in V:
  forall y:
  "Link"(x, y, G) => y in V $

$ "RootsIncluded"(R, V) =
  forall r in R: r in V $

$ "Minimal"(V, R, G) =
  forall x in V: x in "Reach"(R, G) $

$ "FixedPoint"(V, R, G) =
  "RootsIncluded"(R, V)
  and "LinkClosed"(V, G)
  and "Minimal"(V, R, G) $

$ "FixedPoint"(V, R, G)
  => (forall x: x in V => x in "Reach"(R, G)) $

$ "FixedPoint"(V, R, G)
  => (forall x: x in "Reach"(R, G) => x in V) $

$ "Fresh"(x, V, V', G) =
  x in N(G)
  and not (x in V)
  and V' = (x,) + V
  and "Subperm"(V', N(G)) $

$ "Fresh"(x, V, V', G)
  => "size"(V) < "size"(V')
  and "size"(V') <= "size"(N(G)) $

$ B =
  (B_"semantic", B_"scheduling", B_"watchdog") $

$ B_"semantic" != B_"scheduling"
  and B_"scheduling" != B_"watchdog" $

$ C =
  (d_G, g, "scc", "path", d_P,
    b_"semantic-used", b_"scheduling-used", k_"continuation") $

$ "CycleValid"(C)
  and C_"registry" = F_"registry"
  and C_"generation" <= F_"generation"
  => "CycleObserved"(C) $

$ "CycleObserved"(C)
  => not "RetryAuthorizedByObservation"(C) $

$ "Closed"(Q, G, H, F, P, C^*) =
  "RegistryValid"(H_"registry", G)
  and "WitnessValid"(H)
  and "FixedPointReceiptValid"(F)
  and "ProgressReceiptValid"(P)
  and (forall C in C^*: "CycleValid"(C))
  and F_"registry" = H_"registry"
  and F_"roots" = H_"roots"
  and F_"effects" = H_"effects"
  and F_"stable" = "True"
  and F_"pending" = ()
  and "FixedPoint"(F_"visited", F_"roots", G) $
#+end_src

The finite-capacity statement is conditional on an owner-valid
sub-permutation witness.  A raw list with duplicate identities does not
establish termination.

* Evidence state machine

#+begin_src mermaid
stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> RootsSelected: "state=roots-selected / shallow use-composition"
  RootsSelected --> RegistryBound: "evidence=registry-valid / digest + finite identities"
  RegistryBound --> WorklistActive: "state=worklist-active / roots enqueued"
  WorklistActive --> SemanticProgress: "edge=fresh-registered-node / semantic budget consumed"
  SemanticProgress --> WorklistActive: "evidence=node-added / visited remains registry-subperm"
  WorklistActive --> CycleObserved: "edge=visited-node / emit before another scheduling step"
  CycleObserved --> WorklistActive: "state=continue / observation only, no retry authority"
  WorklistActive --> StableCandidate: "state=pending-empty / candidate stable"
  StableCandidate --> ClosureRejected: "check=root-or-link-missing / forged stable"
  StableCandidate --> ClosureRejected: "check=nonminimal / unreachable node included"
  StableCandidate --> ClosureAccepted: "check=roots + link-closed + minimal + owner-valid"
  WorklistActive --> ProgressStalled: "class=stalled|oscillating|regressing"
  WorklistActive --> BudgetExhausted: "owner=semantic|scheduling / separate receipt"
  WorklistActive --> WatchdogObserved: "owner=runtime / wall-clock or runtime anomaly"
  ProgressStalled --> RecoveryEvidence: "evidence=progress receipt + continuation identity"
  BudgetExhausted --> RecoveryEvidence: "evidence=budget-owner receipt"
  WatchdogObserved --> RecoveryEvidence: "evidence=watchdog observation"
  RecoveryEvidence --> RetryBoundary: "authority=runtime-local RetryCapability required"
  RetryBoundary --> WorklistActive: "admission=single-use capability consumed"
  RetryBoundary --> ClosureRejected: "admission=missing|stale|replayed capability"
  ClosureAccepted --> [*]: "terminal=least fixed-point receipt"
  ClosureRejected --> [*]: "terminal=actionable rejection receipt"
#+end_src

The observation is emitted before the next scheduling step so the
offending SCC, causal path, budgets, and continuation identity remain
available even if later recovery fails.

* Owner boundaries

- =gerbil-poo= remains the unique Scheme POO implementation and the
  implementation substrate for pure objects and prototype composition.
- POO Flow defines the mix-module evidence contract and composes
  owner-valid objects; it does not reimplement a generic fixed-point
  library.
- The composition owner binds the finite registry, worklist state, and
  least-closure receipt.
- The progress owner classifies convergence, advance, stall,
  incomparability, regression, oscillation, and budget exhaustion.
- The scheduling owner settles dequeue and dispatch work.
- The runtime watchdog owns time and runtime-anomaly observations.
- The recovery coordinator consumes evidence but cannot resume without
  the runtime-local =RetryCapability=.
- Lean owns countermodels and abstract implications, not runtime
  authenticity.
