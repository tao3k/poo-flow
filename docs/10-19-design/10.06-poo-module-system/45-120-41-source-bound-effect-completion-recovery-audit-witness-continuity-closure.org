* Lean-discovered logic gap

The previous closure proves that every non-committed transition has exactly one
progress owner.  Its aggregate field is:

#+begin_src typst
$
  forall i,
  "active"(i)
  arrow.r.double
  exists o,
  "covered"(e_i, b_i, b_(i+1), o)
$
#+end_src

This statement is sufficient for progress and convergence.  It is not an audit
chain.

The three concrete owner transitions already carry:

- an owner identity;
- a before receipt identity;
- an after receipt identity;
- a recovery identity;
- a proof that the after receipt identity differs from the before receipt
  identity;
- a proof that recovery identity is stable inside the transition.

However, =SourceBoundEffectCompletionRecoveryOwnerCoveredStep= hides those
values inside constructors of a =Prop=.  The aggregate existential keeps only
the owner kind and the covered-step proposition.

Consequently, the previous theorem does not establish:

1. that the after receipt of step =i= is the before receipt of step =i + 1=;
2. that a receipt identity is not reused elsewhere in the recovery chain;
3. that all active steps refer to one non-empty recovery identity;
4. that the exact owner and receipt identities can be projected as first-class
   audit data.

* Countermodels

Local transition closure does not imply chain closure.

#+begin_src typst
$
  t_0 = ("receipt-0", "receipt-1", "recovery-a")
$

$
  t_1 = ("orphan-receipt", "receipt-2", "recovery-b")
$

$
  t_2 = ("receipt-other", "receipt-1", "recovery-a")
$
#+end_src

Every transition can satisfy local non-emptiness and
=afterReceiptIdentity != beforeReceiptIdentity=.  Nevertheless:

#+begin_src typst
$
  "before"(t_1) != "after"(t_0)
$

$
  "after"(t_2) = "after"(t_0)
$

$
  "recovery"(t_1) != "recovery"(t_0)
$
#+end_src

Lean implements these witnesses as:

- =localClosureDoesNotImplyAdjacentContinuity=;
- =localClosureDoesNotImplyGlobalNonReuse=;
- =localClosureDoesNotImplyRecoveryContinuity=.

This is a design defect in the aggregate evidence boundary, not in any of the
three owner-local transition closures.

* Decision

The audit chain owns one receipt-identity function:

#+begin_src typst
$
  r : NN arrow "String"
$

$
  "before"(i) := r(i)
  quad
  "after"(i) := r(i + 1)
$
#+end_src

The after identity of step =i= and the before identity of step =i + 1= are the
same value by construction:

#+begin_src typst
$
  "before"(i + 1) = r(i + 1) = "after"(i)
$
#+end_src

The chain additionally requires =r= to be injective:

#+begin_src typst
$
  forall i, j,
  r(i) = r(j)
  arrow.r.double
  i = j
$
#+end_src

Therefore each transition advances to a distinct receipt generation, and no
receipt generation can appear at two different chain positions.  The equality
between =after(i)= and =before(i + 1)= is the one intentional identity handoff;
it is represented by one position, not by two unrelated receipt fields.

The model is not vacuous.  =canonicalAuditWitnessChain= encodes each natural
position as a non-empty unary String identity.  String-length equality proves
the encoding injective, and =auditWitnessChainModelIsInhabited= constructs an
explicit inhabitant of the strengthened model.

* Type and proposition boundary

The new aggregate
=SourceBoundEffectCompletionRecoveryAuditWitnessEvidence= is a =Type=.

Its first-class audit payload contains:

- =recoveryIdentity : String=;
- =ownerAt : Nat -> ProgressOwner=;
- =ownerIdentityAt : Nat -> String=;
- =receiptIdentityAt : Nat -> String=.

Its laws remain propositions:

- every identity is non-empty;
- =receiptIdentityAt= is injective;
- every active transition is an exact audited owner-covered step;
- scope and provider acknowledgement remain current and stable.

Lean field inspection found a narrower upstream omission: each concrete
=ReceiptClosed= proves that its owner identity and receipt identity are
non-empty, and proves that its recovery identity matches the expectation, but
does not prove that the recovery identity itself is non-empty.  The aggregate
therefore owns =recoveryIdentityPresent= and binds every active concrete receipt
to that one value.

This split is required.  A proposition may certify a fact, but the runtime audit
surface must also project the exact identity values without inventing them
outside the proof boundary.

* Exact binding to the three owners

=SourceBoundEffectCompletionRecoveryAuditedOwnerCoveredStep= binds its indices
directly to the actual after and before receipts.

| Owner | Owner identity | Before identity | After identity | Recovery identity |
|-
| Runtime crash | =afterReceipt.runtimeOwnerIdentity= | =beforeReceipt.receiptId= | =afterReceipt.receiptId= | =afterReceipt.recoveryId= |
| Scheduler | =afterReceipt.schedulerOwnerIdentity= | =beforeReceipt.receiptId= | =afterReceipt.receiptId= | =afterReceipt.recoveryId= |
| Completion storage | =afterReceipt.storageOwnerIdentity= | =beforeReceipt.receiptId= | =afterReceipt.receiptId= | =afterReceipt.recoveryId= |

Each constructor also contains the existing owner-native transition closure and
progress projection.  No parallel transition semantics are introduced.

The following properties are projected from the concrete owner receipts:

- owner identity presence;
- before and after receipt identity presence;
- within-transition receipt identity advance;
- recovery identity equality with the current recovery expectation;
- owner-kind uniqueness.

* Recovery identity continuity

The aggregate carries one non-empty =recoveryIdentity=.  Every active audited
step is indexed by that value, while each owner constructor proves:

#+begin_src typst
$
  rho = rho_i
$
#+end_src

Thus any two active steps share the same recovery identity:

#+begin_src typst
$
  "active"(i) and "active"(j)
  arrow.r.double
  rho_i
  =
  rho_j
$
#+end_src

This prevents a locally valid owner receipt from being admitted into a
different recovery chain.

* Proof projection

The stronger evidence projects to the preceding closure:

#+begin_src typst

  arrow.r
  "OwnerCoverageEvidence"
  arrow.r

The projection intentionally forgets audit identities only after the stronger
properties have been established.  Existing convergence is reused as a theorem;
it is not reimplemented.

* Performance boundary

The proof uses functions from natural-number positions to identities.  It does
not require materializing an infinite collection.  Runtime implementations may
store only the bounded active prefix while preserving the same position and
injectivity contract.

The audit layer adds identity projections and equality checks.  It does not
duplicate fixed-point evaluation, owner scheduling, storage admission, or
recovery execution.

* Remaining proof gap

This closure proves one recovery chain has exact, continuous, globally unique
receipt generations.  It does not yet prove:

- same-owner identity continuity or an authorized identity handoff when the
  progress owner kind changes;
- authorization principal and delegated capability continuity across the same
  receipt chain;
- responsibility and accountability assignment for each owner transition;
- evidence retention and disclosure policy for enterprise audit consumers;
- cross-recovery uniqueness of receipt identities.

The next proof should bind authorization, capability, responsibility, and
evidence disclosure to the exact audit witness chain rather than adding
unrelated enterprise metadata.
