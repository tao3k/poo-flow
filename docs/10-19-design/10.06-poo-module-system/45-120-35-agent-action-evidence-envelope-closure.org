* Owner gap

The project already separates authoritative observation, recovery decision,
evidence roots, and independent declaration bundles. It does not yet have one
Lean-owned envelope that binds those layers for an agent action.

Cursor, Claude, Codex, and future agent hosts expose different local records.
Those records are inputs to source adapters, not proof language. Lean must
receive a normalized, typed projection rather than parse vendor logs.

* Decision

Define =AgentActionEvidenceEnvelope= from:

- =AgentActionIntent=: principal, operation, requested and authorized
  capabilities, declared tool, declared effects, input digest, and previous
  evidence root;
- =AgentObservationEvent=: authoritative action and operation identity,
  resolved tool, granted capabilities, observed effects, evidence roots,
  adapter identity, source-record digest, and provenance;
- =AgentRecoveryDecisionProjection=: decision identity, authoritative
  observation dependency, policy revision, outcome, optional successor root,
  existing =RecoveryDecisionReceipt=, and provenance;
- =BundleSubject=: independently verifiable bundle identity;
- envelope schema, evidence root, bundle digest, and provenance.

* Closure

The envelope closes only when:

#+begin_src typst
$ O."action" = A."action"
  and O."operation" = A."operation"
  and R."action" = A."action"
  and R."observation" = O."event"
  and R."priorRoot" = O."evidenceRoot"
  and O."evidenceRoot" = E."evidenceRoot" $
#+end_src

Every identity, source adapter, source-record digest, evidence root, bundle
digest, and provenance digest must be present. Observation and recovery
validity remain explicit predicates supplied by their owners.

An allowed decision must emit a fresh successor evidence root. Denied,
escalated, and fail-closed decisions emit no successor root in this proof
slice.

* Fail-closed boundaries

- A source-record digest does not prove that its observation projection is
  valid.
- A recovery decision cannot bind a different observation event.
- A populated evidence root cannot replace causal equality.
- A vendor adapter cannot establish policy authority.
- The bundle digest is bound to =BundleSubject= but is not inferred from
  source-record bytes.
- No hidden reasoning trace is required or admitted as proof evidence.
