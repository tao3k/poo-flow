This closure reuses two existing owner modules.

* Lean-discovered adjacency gap

The existing witness adjacency relation requires receipt continuity only when
the owner kind is unchanged:

#+begin_src typst
$
  "owner"(i+1) = "owner"(i)
  arrow.r.double
  "before"(i+1) = "after"(i)
$
#+end_src

When the owner changes, the implication has a false premise and places no
constraint on the receipt handoff.

Lean constructs two valid adjacent witnesses:

#+begin_src typst
$
  w_0 =
  ("runtime", "receipt-0", "receipt-1", "recovery-a")
$

$
  w_1 =
  ("scheduler", "orphan-receipt", "receipt-2", "recovery-a")
$
#+end_src

They satisfy the existing =Adjacent= relation, while:

#+begin_src typst
$
  before(w_1) != after(w_0)
$
#+end_src

The counterexample is
=ownerAuditAdjacencyAllowsCrossOwnerReceiptDiscontinuity=.

* Lean-discovered global non-reuse gap

The existing receipt key is:

#+begin_src typst
$
  "key"(w_i) := ("owner"(i), "after"(i))
$
#+end_src

Therefore two different owners may use the same after receipt identity while
their keys remain unequal:

#+begin_src typst
$
  "after"(w_i) = "after"(w_j)
  quad
  "owner"(i) != "owner"(j)
  quad
  "key"(w_i) != "key"(w_j)
$
#+end_src

Lean proves this with
=ownerPairedReceiptKeyDoesNotDetectCrossOwnerReceiptReuse=.

The enterprise audit contract requires receipt identity uniqueness independent
of the owner currently responsible for progress.

* Decision

The bridge owns no new receipt sequence.  It binds every existing audit witness
to the =receiptIdentityAt= function from 45-120-41:

#+begin_src typst
$
  "beforeReceiptId"(w_i) = r(i)
$

$
  "afterReceiptId"(w_i) = r(i + 1)
$
#+end_src

It also binds:

- witness ordinal to =i=;
- recovery identity to the one audit-chain recovery identity;
- owner kind to =ownerAt i=;
- owner identity to =ownerIdentityAt i=;
- runtime epoch and active fence token to the recovery expectation at =i=.

Every witness remains valid under the existing owner-audit core.  Every
commitment envelope remains closed under the existing commitment scheme.

* Cross-owner continuity

The receipt equality is now unconditional:

#+begin_src typst
$
  "beforeReceiptId"(w_(i+1))
  =
  r(i + 1)
  =
  "afterReceiptId"(w_i)
$
#+end_src

It follows from exact binding, not from the conditional branch in the older
adjacency relation.  The theorem
=auditCommitmentChainOwnerSwitchStillPreservesReceipt= explicitly takes an
owner-change proof and still derives the same continuity equality.

* Global receipt uniqueness

The chain-level identity function is injective:

#+begin_src typst
$
  r(i + 1) = r(j + 1)
  arrow.r.double
  i = j
$
#+end_src

Consequently, two different witness indices cannot share an after receipt
identity, even when their owners differ.

The stronger theorem
=auditCommitmentChainAfterReceiptNotReused= derives this property directly.
=auditCommitmentChainBuildsExistingGlobalNonreuse= then projects it into the
existing owner-paired predicate.

The projection direction matters: owner-independent receipt uniqueness proves
owner-paired uniqueness, but owner-paired uniqueness does not prove
owner-independent receipt uniqueness.

* Commitment payload closure

For every index, the existing
=SourceBoundEffectCompletionRecoveryOwnerAuditCommitmentClosed= proof binds the
commitment payload to the witness.  The bridge composes that proof with the
receipt-chain binding:

#+begin_src typst
$
  "beforeReceiptId"(p_i) = r(i)
$

$
  "afterReceiptId"(p_i) = r(i + 1)
$
#+end_src

=auditCommitmentPayloadBindsExactReceiptChain= proves both equalities.

Existing witness adjacency and existing commitment closure are also reused to
prove:

#+begin_src typst
$
  "previousCommitment"(p_(i+1))
  =
  "some"("commitment"(e_i))
$
#+end_src

This is
=auditCommitmentChainPreservesPredecessorCommitment=.

* Performance boundary

The bridge stores references to existing witness and envelope functions.  It
adds equality proofs and does not hash commitment payloads a second time.

Runtime implementations may materialize the bounded active prefix.  The Lean
model uses total functions so continuity and uniqueness can be stated without
an arbitrary array-size limit.

* Remaining proof gap

This closure binds receipt generations to existing commitment envelopes.  It
does not yet bind every commitment in the chain to:

- =SourceBoundEffectCompletionRecoveryOwnerAuditAuthenticityClosed=;
- the admitted Cedar authorization lifecycle evidence;
- the exact authority grant and bound authority receipt;
- capability continuity or an authorized capability transition;
- responsibility and accountability identities across adjacent commitments.

The next proof must join these existing evidence owners by commitment identity.
It must not introduce another policy engine, grant model, or commitment scheme.
