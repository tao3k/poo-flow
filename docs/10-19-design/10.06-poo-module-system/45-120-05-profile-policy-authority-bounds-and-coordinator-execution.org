#+title: Profile Policy Authority Bounds and Coordinator Execution
#+startup: overview

* Status

The architecture in this RFC is accepted.

This acceptance freezes responsibility and authority boundaries.  It
does not admit implementation files, exported constructor names,
runtime adapters, or a new public macro surface.

* Scope

This RFC defines how recovery policy, recovery authority, Contract
obligations, current-generation evidence, and runtime execution interact
without adding a fifth Module responsibility slot.

It covers:

- recovery policy values selected through =profiles=;
- recovery authority bounds declared through =capabilities=;
- pure authorization before execution;
- coordinator-owned runtime effects;
- successor-generation and receipt obligations.

It does not define a public recovery DSL or grant profiles ambient
runtime authority.

* Central decision

A recovery profile chooses or narrows behavior.  It never grants
authority.

A recovery capability defines an admitted upper bound on actions,
budgets, substitution scope, and continuation use.  It does not execute
an action.

A runtime recovery coordinator validates and materializes one
authorized decision.  It owns runtime handles and effects, but it
cannot invent policy or widen capability authority.

The effective recovery authority is the pure refinement of:

- the active capability evidence;
- the active profile policy restriction;
- the applicable Contract obligations;
- the current evaluation generation;
- the remaining recovery budget and runtime-handle availability.

Failure of any refinement fails closed.

* Placement in the four-slot Module

The four Module responsibilities retain their existing meanings:

| Module responsibility | Recovery contribution                                      |
|-----------------------+------------------------------------------------------------|
| identity              | Identifies the Module and evidence owner                    |
| imports               | Supplies declared reachable Module evidence                 |
| capabilities          | Carries the upper bound of admitted recovery authority      |
| profiles              | Selects and composes recovery policy preferences            |

The runtime coordinator is not a fifth Module slot.  It is an effectful
materialization boundary reached only after semantic authorization.

An imported profile or policy cannot import authority implicitly.
Authority must remain visible as admitted capability evidence in the
composed Module closure.

* RecoveryPolicy responsibility

A recovery policy is a native POO value selected and composed through
=profiles=.  Its semantic responsibilities are:

- inspect an immutable observation projection;
- propose one or more pure =RecoveryDecision= values;
- rank or narrow already permitted alternatives;
- explain why a proposal matches the active profile;
- remain independent of runtime continuation handles and telemetry.

These responsibilities do not automatically freeze a public
=RecoveryPolicy= constructor or macro name.

Profile composition priority resolves policy preference only.  A
higher-priority profile cannot override a capability prohibition,
increase a budget, extend substitution scope, revive an expired
continuation, or authorize a stale generation.

Adding a profile is authority-monotone in the restrictive direction:
it may preserve or reduce the set of executable recovery actions, but
it cannot enlarge that set.

* RecoveryCapability responsibility

A recovery capability is a native POO value in =capabilities=.  Its
semantic evidence may bound:

- admitted recovery action kinds;
- retry and total recovery budgets;
- substitutable responsibilities or result scopes;
- continuation invocation counts;
- eligible generations or recovery-point identities;
- expiration and revocation conditions;
- required Contract or provenance evidence.

Capability composition requires its own explicit admission semantics.
New authority may enter the closure only through an admitted capability
object.  It MUST NOT be synthesized from profile priority, merge order,
defaults, telemetry, or coordinator availability.

* Pure authorization refinement

Authorization is a pure semantic operation.  It consumes evidence and
produces either an admitted action description or an actionable denial.
It performs no retry, substitution, continuation invocation, abort,
telemetry publication, or runtime-handle lookup.

flowchart TD
  O["ObservationEvent for generation G0"] -->|"supplies immutable failure and lineage evidence"| D["RecoveryDecision proposal"]
  P["Active RecoveryPolicy from profiles"] -->|"selects or narrows a permitted proposal"| D
  C["RecoveryCapability from capabilities"] -->|"sets the maximum action and budget authority"| A["Pure authorization refinement"]
  K["Applicable Contract obligations"] -->|"adds contextual proof requirements"| A
  G["Current generation and remaining budget"] -->|"proves freshness and bounded availability"| A
  D -->|"requests one action without executing it"| A
  A -->|"admits an exact action with evidence"| X["Authorized recovery action"]
  A -->|"fails closed with reasons and missing evidence"| N["Recovery denial"]
#+end_src

Authorization MUST verify that:

- the decision source is the current generation;
- the proposing policy belongs to the active composed profile;
- the requested action is within capability bounds;
- all Contract obligations are satisfied;
- budgets and invocation bounds remain available;
- any referenced recovery point remains valid.

* Coordinator execution boundary

The runtime coordinator consumes only an authorized recovery action.
Before effectful execution it revalidates generation identity and
runtime-handle availability.  This closes the race between pure
authorization and runtime materialization.

The coordinator may:

- invoke an admitted ephemeral continuation;
- start an admitted retry;
- materialize an admitted substitution;
- produce an admitted terminal abort;
- publish a redacted execution receipt.

It may not select a different action, widen a budget, substitute a new
policy, fall back to another continuation, or silently restart an
evaluation.

  C -->|"emits a redacted effect and outcome receipt"| R["Coordinator receipt"]
  R -->|"is consumed one-way without granting authority"| T["Telemetry adapter"]

* Profile composition interface

Ordinary users continue to compose profiles:

#+begin_src scheme
(use-composition production-system
  (compose profiles
    (use-module wendao-episteme production)
    resilient-production))
#+end_src

=resilient-production= is a profile value.  It may contain or select a
native POO recovery policy, but it does not carry authority beyond the
composed Module capability closure.

Advanced users extend recovery policy through native =gerbil-poo=
objects, prototypes, slots, composition, and dispatch.  They place the
resulting POO value into profile composition.  The ordinary interface
does not gain an =if= form, recovery clause language, continuation
syntax, or effect hook.

* Denial and receipt requirements

Every denied recovery proposal or execution MUST identify:

- the source generation and decision evidence;
- the rejecting ownership boundary;
- whether the failure concerns policy, capability, Contract,
  generation freshness, budget, or runtime materialization;
- the missing, expired, exhausted, or contradictory evidence;
- the permitted next semantic action, when one exists.

A denial receipt explains failure.  It does not grant a new capability
or trigger an implicit fallback.

* Implementation admission checklist

- [ ] Keep policy selection and restriction in =profiles=.
- [ ] Keep recovery authority bounds in =capabilities=.
- [ ] Prevent every profile, including a higher-priority profile, from
  widening capability authority.
- [ ] Admit new authority only through an explicit capability object.
- [ ] Keep authorization pure and separate from coordinator effects.
- [ ] Validate policy membership, capability evidence, Contract
  obligations, generation freshness, and remaining budgets.
- [ ] Revalidate generation identity and runtime-handle availability at
  the coordinator boundary.
- [ ] Execute exactly the admitted action without silent fallback.
- [ ] Produce a new successor or terminal generation with complete
  parent evidence.
- [ ] Keep continuations ephemeral and coordinator-owned.
- [ ] Publish only redacted coordinator receipts to telemetry.
- [ ] Keep Module at exactly four responsibility slots.
- [ ] Keep the ordinary =use-composition=, =compose profiles=, and
  =use-module= interface unchanged.
- [ ] Add no public recovery DSL, conditional surface, or effect hook.
- [ ] Apply recursive non-widening delegation and conserved budget
  closure in
  [[file:45-120-06-recursive-capability-delegation-and-budget-closure.org][45-120-06]].
