* Decision

This RFC defines one v1 handoff-identity semantics.

A responsibility handoff is globally identified by its recovery identity and
its position in that recovery trace.  Acceptance evidence and effective
observation evidence are execution evidence: each of their identities may name
at most one real handoff key.

A transfer-contract identity and an authority-evidence identity are binding
identities.  They may be shared by multiple handoffs when their owner-supplied
relations validate every use against the exact before and after assignments.
Requiring every identity field to be unique would reject valid enterprise
operation without strengthening execution-evidence safety.

* Lean-discovered architecture gaps

** Blanket uniqueness rejects a valid shared contract

Two different responsibility handoffs may be admitted by the same transfer
contract.  The contract remains valid because each use is related to the exact
predecessor and successor commitments.  Therefore contract identity is not an
execution nonce.

The same distinction applies to authority evidence.  One authority grant or
receipt can authorize several handoffs when its subject, capability, scope, and
freshness predicates hold for each handoff.

** Local validity does not prevent evidence replay

The handoff predicate proves that one transfer is well bound.  It does not
compare that transfer with any other execution.  Two locally valid handoffs can
therefore reuse the same acceptance-evidence or observation-evidence identity
unless a separate nonreuse invariant exists.

** A trace-local natural-number key is not global

Injectivity over a local =Nat= position prevents replay within one recovery
trace but says nothing about another recovery trace.  The actual proof domain
must contain recovery identity:

#+begin_src typst
#let RecoveryId = $cal(R)$
#let Position = $NN$
#let HandoffKey = $cal(K) = cal(R) times NN$

$ k = (r, i) in cal(K) $
#+end_src

This finding changed the Lean core before this RFC was accepted.  The core now
quantifies over an arbitrary =HandoffKey= type; the trace closure instantiates
it as =(recoveryId, position)=.

* Semantic boundary

| Identity class | Required property | Reason |
|----------------------+------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Acceptance evidence | Injective on real global handoff keys | Prevent one acceptance event from acknowledging two executions |
| Effective observation | Injective on real global handoff keys | Prevent one observation from making two transfers effective |
| Transfer contract | Exact relational binding for each use | A contract may govern several transfers |
| Authority evidence | Subject, capability, scope, and freshness validation for each use | One authority decision may authorize several transfers |

Only real handoff positions belong to the injectivity domain.  A stable
responsibility position is outside that domain, so any inert value returned by
=transferAt= at a stable position has no semantic force.

* Mathematical contract

Let =H(k)= mean that the global key =k= denotes a real responsibility handoff.
Let =A(k)= and =O(k)= select the acceptance-evidence and effective-observation
identities of that handoff.

#+begin_src typst
#let H = $H$
#let A = $A$
#let O = $O$
#let K = $cal(K)$

$ forall k_1, k_2 in K,
    H(k_1) and H(k_2) and A(k_1) = A(k_2)
    ==> k_1 = k_2 $

$ forall k_1, k_2 in K,
    H(k_1) and H(k_2) and O(k_1) = O(k_2)
    ==> k_1 = k_2 $
#+end_src

The contrapositive form is the interface used by the closure theorems:

#+begin_src typst
$ forall k_1, k_2 in cal(K),
    H(k_1) and H(k_2) and k_1 != k_2
    ==> A(k_1) != A(k_2) $

$ forall k_1, k_2 in cal(K),
    H(k_1) and H(k_2) and k_1 != k_2
    ==> O(k_1) != O(k_2) $
#+end_src

No injectivity axiom is imposed on transfer-contract or authority-evidence
identity.  Their safety obligation is relational:

#+begin_src typst
#let Binds = $B$
#let Authorized = $U$
#let C = $C$
#let R = $R$
#let Pre = $P_"before"$
#let Post = $P_"after"$
#let Subject = $S$
#let Capability = $Q$
#let Scope = $Sigma$

$ forall k in cal(K),
    H(k) ==> Binds(C(k), Pre(k), Post(k))
      and Authorized(R(k), Subject(k), Capability(k), Scope(k)) $
#+end_src

* POO and proof ownership

The public Scheme surface remains POO-native.  It supplies the ordinary
responsibility-transfer object and the owner relations that bind contract,
authority, acceptance, and observation evidence.  Identity classification is
a proof invariant over those objects; it does not introduce a second user DSL
or a second composition macro.

The Lean ownership split is:

- =HandoffIdentityModel=: independent counterexamples and a canonical valid
  nonreuse witness.
- =HandoffIdentityCore=: key-polymorphic nonreuse predicates and their
  injectivity consequences.
- =HandoffIdentityClosure=: mapping from a local recovery trace to the global
  =(recoveryId, position)= registry while preserving the admitted handoff.

The edge labels distinguish admission, binding, publication, classification,
and nonreuse.  A caller cannot replace one state transition with another merely
because two fields contain equal strings.

* Theorem surface

The core proves:

- distinct real global handoff keys have distinct acceptance-evidence
  identities;
- distinct real global handoff keys have distinct effective-observation
  identities;
- equal acceptance-evidence identities determine the same global handoff key;
- equal effective-observation identities determine the same global handoff
  key.

The trace closure proves:

- a classified changed-responsibility position still has an admitted, exactly
  bound responsibility transfer;
- distinct local positions mapped into the same global registry have distinct
  acceptance and observation evidence;
- adding identity classification preserves the complete handoff evidence
  established by the preceding RFC.

* Remaining implementation obligation

The proof intentionally does not pretend that a process-local function is a
durable enterprise registry.  The runtime owner must publish and retain the
global handoff-key classification across recovery processes, then provide the
Lean-facing relation that its stored entries correspond to
=(recoveryId, position)= and the exact transfer object.

Until that publication owner exists, the theorem is a semantic admission
contract and not a claim of runtime persistence.
