#+title: RFC 149-05 — Effect-Domain Coverage And Compensation Evidence Refinement
#+language: en

* Status

This is a Lean-proven refinement of RFC 149-04.

RFC 149-04 introduces a complete transaction-scoped effect observation.
Its =externalEffectDigest= and uncompensated-application count are not
complete merely because they are populated.  Completeness requires a
plan-bound proof that the observed effect domain covers every effect the
materialization plan can produce.

This refinement defines that coverage proof together with reversible,
compensable, and irreversible effect obligations.  It does not claim
that the Marlin effect-domain registry, effect journal, or compensation
runtime has already landed.

* Discovered logical defects

** Equal observed projections can hide a changed real world

A materialization plan can affect =runtime= and =external-message= while
the declared observation domain contains only =runtime=.  The before and
after projections are then equal even though the external message state
changes.

Lean proves:

#+begin_src text
equalDeclaredProjectionCanHideUncoveredEffect
#+end_src

The defect remains for every digest function over that projection:

#+begin_src text
equalProjectionDigestCannotProveWholeWorldRollback
#+end_src

A cryptographic digest proves identity of its input.  It does not prove
that the input contains every relevant effect.

** A populated coverage witness does not prove domain coverage

A witness can bind the correct materialization-plan digest while its
observed domain omits a plan effect.

Lean proves:

#+begin_src text
populatedCoverageWitnessDoesNotProveCoverage
runtimeOnlyDomainDoesNotCoverPlanUniverse
#+end_src

The owner must prove both the plan-to-universe projection and the
universe-to-observed-domain coverage relation.

** Matching witness fields do not prove owner authority

Lean proves:

#+begin_src text
matchingCoverageFieldsDoNotProveOwnerValidity
#+end_src

The closure retains an independent =EffectCoverageWitnessValid=
relation.  Caller-provided effect IDs are not authoritative plan
analysis.

** A compensation-plan digest does not prove compensation

A compensable effect can contain a non-empty compensation-plan digest
while no compensation receipt exists.

Lean proves:

#+begin_src text
compensationPlanDigestDoesNotProveCompensation
#+end_src

A compensable rollback requires a first-class, owner-valid receipt bound
to the transaction, effect, compensation plan, fence, and restored
pre-effect state.

** One irreversible effect blocks rollback for the entire plan

An irreversible effect cannot be made rollback-safe by compensating
unrelated effects.

Lean proves:

#+begin_src text
irreversibleEffectRejectsWholeRollbackPlan
#+end_src

If any plan effect is irreversible, a =rolledBack= terminal receipt is
forbidden after that effect begins.  The runtime must use an explicit
unresolved or committed outcome according to the owning protocol.

* Lean countermodels and positive theorems

- =equalDeclaredProjectionCanHideUncoveredEffect= constructs two
  different worlds with the same declared projection.
- =equalProjectionDigestCannotProveWholeWorldRollback= generalizes the
  counterexample to every projection digest.
- =runtimeOnlyDomainDoesNotCoverPlanUniverse= proves the missing effect.
- =populatedCoverageWitnessDoesNotProveCoverage= separates plan binding
  from coverage.
- =matchingCoverageFieldsDoNotProveOwnerValidity= preserves the owner
  validity relation.
- =compensationPlanDigestDoesNotProveCompensation= rejects receipt-free
  compensation.
- =irreversibleEffectRejectsWholeRollbackPlan= makes irreversibility
  fail closed.
- =reversibleRuntimeRollbackCloses= constructs one complete reversible
  rollback.
- =coveredRollbackAgreesOnEveryPlanEffect= proves that observed
  agreement covers every effect in the plan universe.
- =closedCompensableRollbackProvidesValidEvidence= extracts a
  transaction-bound compensation receipt from every closed compensable
  obligation.

* Mathematical model

The derived mathematical presentation is Typst:

#+begin_src typst
$ U = (e_1, ..., e_n) $

$ D = (e_"observed-1", ..., e_"observed-m") $

$ pi_D(W) = "projection of effect world W onto D" $

$ exists W_0, W_1:
  (pi_D(W_0) = pi_D(W_1)) and (W_0 != W_1) $

$ "Covers"(U, D) =
  forall e in U: e in D $

$ "WorldComplete"(D, W) =
  forall e in D:
  exists s: (e, s) in W $

$ "WorldFunctional"(W) =
  forall e, s_1, s_2:
  (((e, s_1) in W) and ((e, s_2) in W)) => (s_1 = s_2) $

$ "Agree"(D, W_0, W_1) =
  forall e in D:
  forall s:
  ((((e, s) in W_0) => ((e, s) in W_1))
  and (((e, s) in W_1) => ((e, s) in W_0))) $

$ "Compensates"(Q, o, W_0, C) =
  exists r in C:
  "CompensationReceiptValid"(r)
  and (r_"transaction" = Q_"transaction")
  and (r_"effect" = o_"effect")
  and (r_"plan" = o_"compensation-plan")
  and (r_"restored" = "BeforeState"(W_0, o_"effect")) $

$ "RollbackObligation"(Q, o, W_0, C) = cases(
  "True" & "if reversible",
  "Compensates"(Q, o, W_0, C) & "if compensable",
  "False" & "if irreversible",
) $

$ "RollbackClosed"(Q, H, O, C, W_0, W_1) =
  "CoverageWitnessValid"(H)
  and (H_"plan" = Q_"materialization-plan")
  and "Covers"(H_"universe", H_"domain")
  and "ObligationsCover"(H_"universe", O)
  and "WorldValid"(W_0)
  and "WorldValid"(W_1)
  and "WorldComplete"(H_"domain", W_0)
  and "WorldComplete"(H_"domain", W_1)
  and "WorldFunctional"(W_0)
  and "WorldFunctional"(W_1)
  and "Agree"(H_"domain", W_0, W_1)
  and (forall o in O: "RollbackObligation"(Q, o, W_0, C)) $

$ "Covers"(U, D) and "Agree"(D, W_0, W_1)
  => "Agree"(U, W_0, W_1) $
#+end_src

The model separates four identities:

- the materialization plan;
- the plan's possible effect universe;
- the domain actually observed by the journal;
- the concrete before and after effect worlds.

No digest may collapse those identities into one untyped string.
RFC 149-06 provides the normative transitive =use-composition=
derivation of the plan effect universe.

* Plan-bound effect universe

=EffectCoverageWitness= contains:

- witness ID;
- exact materialization-plan digest;
- effect universe;
- observed effect domain.

The materialization owner derives the effect universe from the exact
plan.  The universe includes every direct and indirect effect that the
plan can initiate, including:

- local runtime state;
- filesystem and database mutations;
- messages and notifications;
- remote service mutations;
- credentials, capabilities, and authority exercises;
- workflow, knowledge, policy, authentication, and evidence mutations;
- spawned or delegated work that remains attributable to the
  transaction.

An effect delegated to another component does not leave the universe.
It changes the receipt owner.

* Coverage and world completeness

Coverage requires:

#+begin_src text
every effect in the plan universe is in the observed domain
#+end_src

Each before and after world must:

- contain a state fact for every observed effect;
- contain at most one state fact for each effect ID;
- satisfy the owner-defined =EffectWorldValid= relation.

=worldsAgreeOn= compares full effect facts.  With a valid coverage
witness, Lean proves that agreement on the observed domain entails
agreement on every plan effect.

* Reversibility obligations

Every effect in the plan universe has exactly one obligation:

- =reversible=: equality of complete before and after observations is
  sufficient under the effect owner's semantics;
- =compensable=: rollback additionally requires a valid compensation
  receipt;
- =irreversible=: rollback is unavailable after the effect begins.

=obligationsCoverUniverse= prevents an effect from disappearing merely
because no reversibility declaration was supplied.  It also rejects
obligations for effects outside the plan universe.

* Compensation receipt contract

=CompensationReceipt= contains:

- receipt ID;
- transaction ID;
- effect ID;
- compensation-plan digest;
- fence token;
- restored-state digest.

For a compensable obligation, closure requires a receipt that:

- is accepted by =CompensationReceiptValid=;
- binds the canonical transaction;
- binds the exact effect and compensation plan;
- was produced under the active recovery fence;
- restores the state recorded for that effect in the complete
  pre-effect world.

The fence token is part of the receipt schema.  The concrete runtime
projection must additionally prove equality with the active RFC 149-04
recovery fence.

* Effect-domain state machine

#+begin_src mermaid
stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> PlanBound: "state=plan-bound / exact materialization plan digest"
  PlanBound --> UniverseDerived: "state=universe-derived / all direct + delegated effects enumerated"
  UniverseDerived --> CoverageRejected: "coverage=missing / observed domain omits a plan effect"
  UniverseDerived --> CoverageValid: "coverage=valid / owner witness + universe subset domain"
  CoverageValid --> Prepared: "state=prepared / complete functional before-world durable"
  Prepared --> Applying: "state=applying / RFC 149-04 fence valid"
  Applying --> Committed: "outcome=committed / complete after-world valid"
  Applying --> RollbackCheck: "outcome=rollback-requested / inspect every obligation"
  RollbackCheck --> Compensating: "mode=compensable / bound receipt required"
  Compensating --> RolledBack: "receipt=valid / full world restored"
  RollbackCheck --> RolledBack: "mode=reversible / full world restored"
  RollbackCheck --> Unresolved: "mode=irreversible / rollback forbidden"
  CoverageRejected --> [*]: "terminal=rejected / no effect begins"
  Committed --> [*]: "terminal receipt valid"
  RolledBack --> [*]: "terminal receipt valid"
  Unresolved --> [*]: "terminal evidence names uncompensated effect"
#+end_src

Every transition names the proof state.  =Unresolved= is explicit
evidence of an incomplete compensation boundary; it must never be
relabeled =rolledBack=.

* Owner boundaries

- Marlin owns materialization-plan analysis, effect-universe projection,
  journal coverage, effect worlds, fence-bound compensation, and runtime
  receipts.
- The owner of an external effect validates its state and compensation
  semantics.
- Lean owns the coverage countermodels and abstract closure.
- POO Flow composes the evidence objects and fails closed when any effect
  lacks coverage, reversibility classification, or owner validity.
